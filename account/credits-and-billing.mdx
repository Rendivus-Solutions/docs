Credits are the currency for AI generation in Raddivus. You receive a monthly allowance based on your plan — **50 on Starter, 200 on Professional, and 2,000 on VIP** — and you can buy more at any time. See [Plans and credits](/getting-started/plans-and-credits) for the full comparison.

## Rollover and expiration

- **Unused monthly credits roll over** with no cap. Credits from previous months stay in your balance indefinitely.
- **Purchased credits never expire.**
- When credits run out, generation is blocked until you buy more or your next cycle adds new credits.
- If a generation fails, the credits are **refunded automatically**. That includes one that never finishes: an Instagram post still being written, or an Instagram image still generating, after 10 minutes is stopped, marked failed with a "try again" message, and its credits are returned within about 5 minutes.

## Deduction order

Monthly credits are used first. Purchased credits are used only after your monthly credits are exhausted.

## Billing rules

- **Upgrade mid-cycle:** you receive the difference in credits immediately. Upgrading from Starter to Professional adds 150 credits right away.
- **Monthly or yearly:** yearly plans are billed once for the year and cost the equivalent of 10 months, so you get 2 months free. Your credit allowance is still granted monthly.
- **Downgrade:** takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep your current plan until then.
- **Cancellation:** access continues until the period ends. Purchased credits remain usable. Monthly grants stop.
- **Payment failure:** a grace period of about 7 days allows retries. If all retries fail, the account is canceled and unused monthly credits are frozen until reactivated.
