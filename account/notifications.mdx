---

Raddivus notifies you when something you started in the background finishes, such as an Instagram visual or a video.

## Where notifications appear

- **The bell icon** in the top-right header lights up with a red dot when something is ready. Click it to see your recent events.
- **Toasts** appear in the bottom-right of any screen, so you are notified no matter which page you are on.

## How it works

The bell keeps your last 20 events for quick review. When you start something that runs in the background, like approving an Instagram idea, you can keep working anywhere in the app and the notification finds you when the result is ready. Events older than 30 days are cleared automatically.

<Tip>Notifications are private to you. You only ever see your own events.</Tip>
