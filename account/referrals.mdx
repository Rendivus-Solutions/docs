title: "Refer a friend"
description: "Share your referral link to earn credits when friends sign up and start paying. Earn up to a full month of free credits per referral."
---

Your referral link is in **Settings → Referrals** (also on the sidebar's "Refer a friend" card). Share it with anyone who might benefit from Raddivus.

## How referrals work

When your friend signs up with your link:

- **They get a 14-day free trial** instead of 7 days.
- **You both earn credits** once they complete their first full month on a paid plan. Each person gets their own plan's monthly credit amount as purchased credits (for example, a Professional referrer earns 200 credits; a Starter friend earns 50). These credits never expire.
- **Rewards stack.** Refer as many friends as you want—there's no cap.

## Track your referrals

The Referrals tab shows the status of each referral:

- **Signed up** — Your friend created an account.
- **Subscribed** — They've started a paid plan.
- **Rewarded** — Their first month ended and you both earned your credits.

Hover or tap any status to see what happens next and when your credits are due. A preview of your friend's email is hidden for privacy. Your totals appear at a glance: registrations, pending credits, and total earned.

You get a bell notification, an in-app message, and an email when a friend signs up. The Referrals tab refreshes automatically while open.

## Eligibility and rules

- Referrals are **free on every plan**, including during your trial.
- Your friend must **stay subscribed for their full first month**. If they cancel or their payment fails before then, neither of you gets a reward.
- **Yearly plans unlock the reward** after the first paid month completes—you don't wait a full year.
- To **receive your reward**, you need an active paid plan when it unlocks. If you're on trial when your friend qualifies, the reward waits until you subscribe.
- If your friend's payment is later **refunded or disputed**, both referral credits reverse. Referral credits are promotional and excluded from the satisfaction guarantee.
- **You can't refer yourself** by signing up with your own link, email, or payment method.
