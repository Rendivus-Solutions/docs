Settings is the control center for your account. Open it from the sidebar.

## The tabs

| Tab                 | What it does                                                    |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Profile**         | Update your name and avatar                                     |
| **Personalization** | Set defaults, configure agent memory, and import from other AI  |
| **Security**        | Change your password                                            |
| **Connections**     | Connect Meta, Instagram, and ClickUp                            |
| **Usage**           | See your generation counts per tool                             |
| **Team**            | Manage members and invitations (VIP)                            |
| **Credits**         | View your balance and buy top-up packs                          |
| **Billing**         | View your plan, status, and billing dates                       |
| **Appearance**      | Switch between light and dark mode                              |
| **Referrals**       | Share your link and track rewards from friends who convert      |
