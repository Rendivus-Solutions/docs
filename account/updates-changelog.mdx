The **Updates** page (sidebar → Updates) is where you follow what is new in Raddivus. It is free to view on all plans.

## The three tabs

- **Shipped:** features we have recently launched.
- **In progress:** what we are building now.
- **Planned:** the roadmap of what is coming.

Each update shows a title, a short summary, and a category (feature, improvement, fix, or beta). Some updates are tagged with the tool they relate to.

## Announcements

When we ship something notable, an announcement appears the next time you log in, and the bell icon shows a dot until you visit the Updates page.

<Tip>
  VIP members also get early feature access and announcements in the private
  Slack channel.
</Tip>
