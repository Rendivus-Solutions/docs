The **Updates** page (sidebar → Updates) is where you follow what is happening in Raddivus. It is free to view on all plans.

## The three tabs

- **Shipped:** features that have launched.
- **In progress:** features being built.
- **Planned:** the roadmap of upcoming work.

Each update shows a title, a short summary, and a category (feature, improvement, fix, or beta). Some updates are tagged with the tool they relate to.

## Announcements

When a notable feature ships, an announcement appears the next time you log in, and the bell icon shows a dot until you visit the Updates page.

<Tip>
  VIP members also get early feature access and announcements in the private
  Slack channel.
</Tip>
