---
title: "The approval system"
description: "The AI Agent never changes your account without permission. Every modification shows an approval card you accept or reject."
---

The agent never makes changes without explicit permission. The agent proposes, and you decide.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="The agent explains">
    It describes what it wants to do and why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="An approval card appears">
    A card in the chat shows the exact action details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You decide">Click **Approve** or **Reject**.</Step>
  <Step title="It executes">
    If approved, the action runs immediately, and the result (success or
    failure) appears in the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The agent carries on">
    A few seconds later the agent replies by itself: it reads the outcome and
    proposes the next step or tells you the result. Until the reply arrives,
    "Working on the next step" shows under the card. You do not need to type
    "continue", and it happens even if you close the tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If another campaign, ad set or ad in your account has the same name as the one
on the card, the card also shows its Meta ID under the name, so you can tell
which one you are approving. For an upload, it shows the ID of the ad set the
ad goes into. The agent knows this from the lists it read while preparing the
proposal.

Rejecting a card does nothing further. The agent's follow-up reply is billed
like any other agent reply.

## Pausing an ad, and undoing it

A card that pauses an ad shows the ad itself: its picture, verdict and current
numbers, and **Open ad**. While the pause is still to come, it also says
roughly how much a day the pause stops spending, when the budget is known.
After it runs, the card's heading says **Ad paused**.

**Undo** turns the ad back on with one click, no second approval. It works for
24 hours after the pause, and only while the ad is still paused on Meta: if
you or a teammate changed the ad in Ads Manager since, Undo changes nothing and
says so. After an undo, the heading says **Ad running**. Past 24 hours, ask the
agent to resume the ad; it proposes that on a normal approval card.

Approval cards are saved with the conversation. Reopen a chat in another tab,
on another device or from the history list and each card is where it was, with
its current status; a card still waiting can be approved from there.

The card appears below the agent's reply. Each approval runs **exactly once**:
clicking **Approve** twice, or in two tabs, does not repeat the change on Meta
or charge you twice. A budget change is applied to the exact cent you approved.

The agent works in your ad account's own currency, the same as the Ad Analytics
page. Spend, budgets and costs it reads out carry that currency's symbol, and a
budget it proposes is in that currency: ¥3,000 on a yen account reaches Meta as
¥3,000. If the account's currency changed after the proposal, approving it
changes nothing and the agent asks for a new proposal.

The agent never assumes a currency. If your ad account's currency is not on
record yet, the agent looks it up from Meta. If Meta cannot say, amounts are
shown without a currency symbol and the agent will not propose or apply a
budget change until the currency is known.

## When the agent asks you to choose

Sometimes the agent needs a decision before it can go on, such as which
creative an ad should use. It then asks a question with lettered answers, shown
in place of the message box until you answer. Click an answer (or press its
letter), or press **Type my own answer** to write something else. Once
answered, the question folds to one line in the chat, such as "Which creative
should the ad use? A video I give you". Click it to see every answer with your
pick marked.

When the agent needs several answers in a row, the message box numbers them:
"Question 2 of 3" when the agent said up front how many it needs, or
"Question 2" when it did not. A single question has no number.

On "Question 2 of 3", the back arrow reopens question 1 with its answers and
your earlier pick marked. Pick a different answer and the agent is told you
changed it and carries on from there. Pick the same answer, or press the
forward arrow, and you are back on question 2 with nothing sent.

A question is not an approval: your pick is sent as your next message and
nothing changes on your account. Answering costs nothing; the agent's next
reply is billed like any other.

## What needs approval

Approval is required for: pausing or resuming campaigns and ads, changing budgets, generating images, creating variations, and uploading creatives to Meta.

## What runs automatically

Reading campaign data, viewing stats, browsing generated ads, and listing folders all run automatically without approval.

<Note>
  When you upload a generated ad to Meta, it is always created in **paused**
  status. It will not spend any budget until you activate it yourself in Meta
  Ads Manager. This prevents accidental spend from any upload.
</Note>
