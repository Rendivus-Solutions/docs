**Plans:** Professional and VIP. **Cost:** building is free; the chat message
that asks for it is billed like any agent reply.

## What the agent will build

Each step is its own [approval card](/ai-agent/approvals), and everything is
created **paused** in Ads Manager. Nothing spends until you approve a separate
card that turns the campaign on, and that card says how much.

- A new **campaign**, **ad set** or **ad** (from an existing ad's creative, a
  Raddivus image, or a video already in your ad account), a rename, or a daily
  budget change.
- **Copying an ad** into another ad set: same picture or video, text and button.
- **Several ads from one picture or video** (1 to 5, three by default), each
  with different copy written from your brand profile. The card lists every
  headline before you approve.
- **Boosting an Instagram post** that Meta lets you promote. The card shows the
  daily budget and how many days it runs.
- **Audiences**: a lookalike of an existing audience, or the people who engaged
  with your Facebook Page or Instagram account. Customer-list uploads are not
  available.

The agent checks every ad, ad set, Page, video, post and audience it names
against your ad account before a card appears.

## How it decides

- A new goal gets a new campaign; a new audience or budget gets a new ad set; a
  new picture, video or copy gets a new ad.
- It copies a winning ad rather than editing it, because editing resets what
  Meta has learned about the ad.
- It only copies an ad into a campaign with the same goal (Meta refuses
  anything else).
- It never proposes a new ad set or ad where Meta has stopped delivering. It
  tells you what Meta reported and how to fix it instead.

## Meta's own diagnostics

Without an approval card and at no extra cost, the agent can answer from
Meta's own tools: why an ad is not delivering, who changed what and when,
Meta's Opportunity Score and its suggestions, how your results compare with
your industry, how a metric is trending, whether anything looks unusual, what
other advertisers run (the Ad Library), and how Meta itself works (with the
Help Center article linked). When Meta has no benchmark or trend for your
account yet, the agent says so rather than estimating one.

Ask to see an ad and the agent links Meta's preview of each placement. Each
link is a small chip with the Instagram or Facebook logo and the placement name
("Instagram Feed"), and it opens Meta's preview in a new tab.

## Reconnecting

Building needs a fresh Meta connection. When an ad account needs one, the
agent's chat box says **Reconnect needed to build ads**: reconnect it in
[Connect Meta Ads](/connections/connect-meta). Reports keep working in the
meantime.
