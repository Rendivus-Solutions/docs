The agent offers three AI tiers. You select the tier from a dropdown in the chat header, and you can switch at any time, even mid-conversation. Tiers are tied to your plan.

| Tier              | Available on | Best for                                     |
| ----------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Raddivus Lite** | All plans    | Quick lookups and simple tasks               |
| **Raddivus**      | Growth, VIP  | Complex analysis and multi-step reasoning    |
| **Raddivus Pro**  | VIP only     | Deep audits, strategy, and parallel research |

## Raddivus Lite

Fast responses for everyday tasks. Use it for a quick answer or a simple action like pausing a campaign or generating a few ads.

Example requests: "What's my CTR this week?", "Pause campaign X", "Show me my top 5 ads by spend."

## Raddivus

The same capabilities as Lite, with significantly smarter reasoning. Better at connecting patterns across your account, spotting the causes behind metric changes, and handling multi-step requests.

Example requests: "Why did my return drop this week?", "Compare all my campaigns and tell me where to reallocate budget."

## Raddivus Pro

Everything in Raddivus, plus three capabilities that activate for complex requests: extended thinking, task planning, and sub-agent delegation. See [Pro features](/ai-agent/pro-features).

Example requests: "Audit my entire account and give me a full optimization plan", "Analyze all my ads, find patterns in the top performers, and generate new ads based on what's working."

## When to use Pro vs Raddivus

Use **Raddivus** when the question has a clear, bounded answer, like this week's performance or a single ad set's stats. Use **Pro** when the request needs data from multiple places, a strategy, or a recommendation that depends on patterns across your whole account. The extra thinking is worth it when depth matters more than speed.
