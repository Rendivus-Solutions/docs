The AI Agent is a conversational assistant connected to your Meta Ads account. You ask questions and give instructions in plain language. It reads your campaign data, explains what is happening, and can make changes to your account, always with your approval first.

**Where:** sidebar → **Agent**. **Cost:** free to chat (some actions like image generation cost 1 credit). **Plans:** Professional and VIP (Starter does not include the AI Agent).

Recommended reading order: start with [Permissions](/ai-agent/permissions), then [Approvals](/ai-agent/approvals), then [Model tiers](/ai-agent/model-tiers), and finally [Pro features](/ai-agent/pro-features). To see how the agent learns about you over time, read [What the agent remembers](/ai-agent/memory).

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a Meta account">
    Open the Agent and select an ad account. If you have not connected one,
    follow [Connect Meta Ads](/connections/connect-meta).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question">
    Type something like "How are my campaigns doing this week?" or "Which ad set
    has the worst CPC?" The agent already knows your brand profile, so it never
    asks basic questions about your business.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve actions">
    When the agent proposes a change, it shows an approval card. Nothing happens
    until you click Approve. See [The approval system](/ai-agent/approvals).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you connect an ad account

You can use the agent before any ad account is connected. A new chat opens on the brand you have selected, with ideas for what it can do right away: make ads, write ad copy, find angles, and plan a launch.

- **Making ads works as usual.** Ask for "3 ads for my $49 offer" and the agent proposes them on an approval card. Images cost 1 credit each, after you click Approve.
- **Publishing and results come with an ad account.** Ask the agent to publish an ad, or how your ads are doing, and it tells you what it can do now and shows a **Connect ad account** button under its reply. The button opens **Settings → Connections** over the chat, so you come back to the same conversation. Connecting takes about 2 minutes. See [Connect Meta Ads](/connections/connect-meta).
- **It never makes up numbers.** With no ad account there are no campaigns or results to read, and the agent says so instead of guessing.

These chats are listed under your brand's name in the chat list. After you connect an ad account, new chats let you pick it; earlier chats stay about the brand.

## What it can do

**Read and analyze (automatic, no approval needed):** campaign overviews, performance (spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, reach, frequency), ad set and ad data, account-level insights, and your brand context. Ask whether an ad is working and the agent judges it with the same rules as your weekly recommendations, and tells you if the verdict changed since the weekly review.

**Modify your account (requires approval):** pause or resume campaigns and ads, and change campaign budgets.

**Generate creatives (requires approval, costs credits):** generate new ad images (1 credit each) with the same engine as the [Ad Generator](/create-ads/ad-generator), create variations of an existing ad (1 credit each) like the [Ad Copier](/create-ads/ad-copier), and upload creatives to Meta (free). Uploaded ads are always created **paused**.

## How answers are laid out

Answers are written to be skimmed in a few seconds.

- **A review starts with the verdict.** Ask how your ads are doing and the first line says what is happening and the one move to make. Then come short sections: Recommended action, What is working, What is not working, Too early to judge, and Bigger picture. Empty sections are left out.
- **Ads show as cards.** Each ad the answer talks about appears as a card with its picture, name, verdict, spend, results, cost per result, CTR, and the reason for its verdict. The numbers come straight from your Meta account, never typed by the AI.
- **Pick the period and the numbers.** Above the cards, **Last 7 days** switches the numbers to the last 7, 14 or 30 days, and the cost per result shows how it moved against the period before (for example "-30% vs the 7 days before"; a new ad says "New, no earlier period"). **Numbers** chooses what the cards show: budget, spend, results, cost per result, CTR and CPC. Your choices are remembered on this browser, and teammates keep their own. The verdict itself is always judged on the last 30 days against similar ads; point at it to see that cost. **Open ad** opens an ad Raddivus made in Raddivus, and any other ad in Meta Ads Manager in a new tab.
- **Terms explain themselves.** Marketing terms such as CTR, CPM, ROAS, ad set and learning phase are underlined with dots the first time they appear. Point at one, or tab to it with the keyboard, to read what it means.
- **Names stand out.** Campaign, ad set and audience names show as grey name tags, spelled exactly as in Meta.
- **Background is folded.** Detail you can skip, like how the agent worked something out, sits under **More context**. Click it to open.

In the chat box, start a line with `- ` or `1. ` to write a list.

## Pointing the agent at your projects

Type `@` and pick a [project](/create-ads/projects) to hand the agent that project's name and custom instructions.

## Copying a message keeps its mentions

Copy a message you sent (the copy icon under it, or select part of its text) and paste it into the chat box of any conversation: every mention comes back as a chip, including folders, Knowledge Base documents, projects, and Web search. Pasted anywhere else, it reads as plain text with each mention written as `@Name`. Uploaded files are not part of the copy.

## Editing your last message

Asked something the wrong way? Hover your most recent message and click the pencil beside the copy icon. The message reopens in the chat box with its text and any highlighted references intact, so you can reword it and send again.

**Both versions are kept.** Small arrows appear under the message showing `1/2`, `2/2`, and so on. Moving between them shows that version's question **and** the answer it produced, so nothing you asked is thrown away. The version you are currently on is the one the agent remembers going forward, so a badly worded question stops affecting the rest of the conversation.

Two things to know:

- Only your **most recent** message can be edited, and only once the agent has finished replying. Older messages keep their copy button. Once you send a new message, the older turn settles on the version you left it on.
- **Files are not re-attached.** If the message you are editing had files uploaded to it, the chat box tells you so. Re-attach them before sending if you still want the agent to read them. Mentions of folders and [Knowledge Base](/brands/knowledge-base) documents, and the Web search toggle, all come back automatically.

Editing costs nothing by itself. Sending the edited message is a normal turn, billed exactly like any other message, and moving between versions you already have is free.

## Examples

- "How are my campaigns doing this week?"
- "Which ad set has the worst CPC?"
- "Pause the campaign that's spending the most with the lowest return."
- "Generate 3 new ad images for my landscaping business."
