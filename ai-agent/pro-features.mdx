---
title: "Raddivus Pro features"
description: "The Pro tier adds extended thinking, task planning, and sub-agent delegation for deep audits and strategy. Available on VIP."
---

Raddivus Pro (on the VIP plan) adds three capabilities on top of the Raddivus tier. They activate for complex requests.

## Extended thinking

The agent works through problems visibly before responding. You see a "Thinking..." indicator in real time, and when it finishes it shows how long it thought. You can expand the panel to read the full reasoning or collapse it. This makes the agent's conclusions easier to trust and verify.

It is especially useful for audits and strategic questions, where you want to understand the reasoning, not just the conclusion.

## Task planning

For multi-step requests, the agent creates a numbered plan before executing. Each step shows a status (pending, running, done, or failed) that updates as it works, so you see exactly what it is doing and in what order. For example:

1. Pull campaign performance, done
2. Analyze ad set spend efficiency, running
3. Review creative performance, pending
4. Identify optimization opportunities, pending
5. Build final recommendations, pending

## Sub-agent delegation

The agent can hand off data-gathering tasks to a faster assistant that runs in parallel. Steps handled this way show a "Sub-Agent" badge. The main agent collects all results and synthesizes them into a single recommendation, which makes comprehensive account audits faster without sacrificing depth.

## When to reach for Pro

Use Pro for requests like "Audit my entire account and give me a full optimization plan" or "Create a campaign strategy for next month based on what's worked so far." For quick, bounded questions, the Raddivus or Lite tiers are faster and cost less per message.
