Ad Analytics is a visual gallery of every ad in your connected Meta Ads accounts. Use it as a quick audit of every active and historical creative you have run.

**Where:** sidebar → **Ad Analytics**. **Cost:** free to view. Requires a [connected Meta account](/connections/connect-meta).

<img src="/images/concept-analytics.svg" alt="Illustration of live ad performance metrics and charts" noZoom />

## What you see

Each ad shows its thumbnail, status, and key metrics pulled live from Meta:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- Spend
- CTR (click-through rate)
- CPC (cost per click)
- Reach

## Weekly recommendations

Every week, Raddivus judges each running Meta ad: **winning**, **holding steady**, **underperforming**, or **too early to judge**. For a winning or underperforming ad it names the most likely reason (the angle, the message, the visual, the copy, the audience, or for videos the first 3 seconds, the middle, or the ending) and proposes 1 to 5 tests to run next, such as a new creative or a new angle. Each recommendation cites a real number from your account, never a vague suggestion.

Ads that are too new or have too little data never get a verdict. They are grouped into one **Give it time** note, which you can dismiss. An ad is judged once it has run 3 days and has 1,000 views plus 3 results, or has spent twice your usual cost per result for that kind of result.

The Ad Analytics grades, the weekly recommendations and the [AI agent](/ai-agent/overview) all use these same rules, so they give the same verdict about the same ad.

Apply the recommendations you want, or dismiss the rest. Applying queues it for execution. You can also view your past decisions — accepted and dismissed items are tracked so future analyses never repeat something you've already decided on.

Weekly recommendations are free on every plan, including the trial. They require a connected, active Meta ad account. 0 credits.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No ads are showing">
    The account may have no ads, or the filters are too narrow. Clear the
    filters and confirm the account has campaigns in Meta.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A video or carousel won't show a full preview">
    Some ad formats only return a preview thumbnail. View the original in Meta
    Ads Manager.
  </Accordion>
