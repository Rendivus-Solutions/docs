The Ad Library is a browsable gallery of ads you can use for inspiration. Browse it for ideas, save ads to your folders, or send one to the Copier to make your own branded version.

**Where:** sidebar → **Ad Library**. **Cost:** free to browse and save.

## Browse and filter

Scroll the gallery, search by keyword, and filter by attributes like industry, media type, ad angle, tone, and format. Click any ad to open a detail view with its full image and metadata.

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Save to a folder" icon="bookmark">
    Hover an ad and save it to one of your [folders](/collaborate/saved-ads) for
    later.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send to the Copier" icon="copy" href="/create-ads/ad-copier">
    Open an ad and send it to the Copier, where it loads as the source for
    branded variations.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can also select several ads at once to save or download them in bulk.

<Note>
  Your own generated ads can be shared to the library from the Generator and
  Copier results screens, using the **Share** action in the selection bar.
</Note>
