The Ad Library is a browsable catalogue of finished ads. Browse it for ideas, save ads to folders, download them, or send one to the Ad Copier to create your own branded variation. Free on every plan — no credits to browse, save, or download. Credits are only spent if you take an ad into the Ad Copier and generate from it.

**Where:** sidebar → **Ad Library**. **Cost:** free to browse and save.

## Browsing and filters

Four filters sit in the bar at the bottom of the gallery:

- **Style** — what the ad looks like at a glance: Minimal, Bold, Photographic, Illustrated, Type-led, Screenshot, or Soft.
- **Industry** — the business the ad is advertising.
- **Ad type** — the creative format: Before & After, Testimonial, Problem–Solution, Apple Notes Style, and ten more.
- **More** — media type and aspect ratio, tucked away because they are production questions rather than browse questions.

Every option shows how many ads match it, counted against whatever else you've already picked. Options with no ads are shown greyed out rather than hidden, so you can see where the catalogue is thin instead of guessing.

Your first visit is filtered to your active brand's industry, with a **Browse everything** link to step out of it.

### Empty combinations

If a combination has no ads, the page widens instead of going blank. It keeps whichever filter you picked most recently and loosens the others, then tells you what it loosened: "No ads in the Minimal style in Roofing. Showing 12 ads in the Minimal style from every industry."

## Using an ad

**Use this ad** appears on every card and in the detail view. It opens the Ad Copier with the image already loaded, the aspect ratio already set, and the ad's type, angle, style and industry carried across and shown above the form. Your own brand details are not overwritten — the source ad's industry travels as context about the ad, not as a change to your brand.

You can also save an ad to a folder, copy it to the clipboard, or download it, individually or several at once.

## Sharing an ad to the library

Any ad you have generated can be shared to the library from your saved ads.

Sharing is free and immediate — there is no approval queue. There is a limit of 10 shared ads per person per day.

Ads are hosted by Raddivus. An ad cannot point at an image stored anywhere else, which makes it possible to remove one later.
