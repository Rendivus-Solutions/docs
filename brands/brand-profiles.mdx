title: "Brand profiles"

---

A brand profile stores the details every tool needs to create on-brand content, so you never re-enter them. Brands are **free** on all plans, and you can create as many as you want and switch between them per generation.

## What you can store

- **Brand name** (required)
- **Industry** (required)
- **Tagline** or short description
- **Website** and social links
- **Logo** (uploaded image)
- **Up to five brand colors**
- **Services** you offer

## Create a brand

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Brands">
    Click **Brands** in the sidebar, then **Add brand**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basics">
    Enter the brand name and pick an industry. These are the only required
    fields. Add a tagline, website, logo, and colors if you have them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create brand**. It is available to every tool immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The brand overview

Open any brand card to see its overview:

- **Completeness bar** showing how filled-in the brand is across identity, visuals, offering, reach, and context.
- **Identity & colors** with your typography samples and color swatches.
- **Web & social** with inline-editable links. Handle-style platforms (Instagram, X, TikTok) take a username, and the @ is added for you. URL-style platforms (website, Facebook, LinkedIn) take a full URL.
- **Services** as a list of pills. Click the plus pill, type a service, and press Enter. Click the × on a pill to remove it.

## How the AI uses your brand

When you generate ads through the [Generator](/create-ads/ad-generator), [Copier](/create-ads/ad-copier), or [AI Agent](/ai-agent/overview), Raddivus reads your brand profile automatically. The agent already knows your name, industry, services, and colors before you type anything, so it never asks basic questions about your business.

## Edit or delete

Open a brand card and click **Edit** to change anything, including replacing or removing the logo and adding or removing colors. To delete, use the menu on the brand card and confirm. Deleting a brand also removes its knowledge base and any folders linked to it, so it cannot be undone.

<Note>
  For deeper context that shapes the AI's writing voice, add documents and files
  to the brand's [Knowledge Base](/brands/knowledge-base).
</Note>
