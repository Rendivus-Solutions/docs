---

A brand profile, also called a Brand Kit, stores the details every tool needs to create on-brand content, so you never re-enter them. Creating and using a brand costs **no credits** on any plan, and you can switch between your brands per generation.

Your plan sets how many Brand Kits you can keep: **1 on Starter, 3 on Professional, and 7 on VIP**. See [Plans and credits](/getting-started/plans-and-credits).

## What you can store

- **Brand name** (required)
- **Industry** (required)
- **Tagline** or short description
- **About**: what the business is, in your own words
- **Website** and social links (Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube)
- **Logo** (uploaded image)
- **Wordmark**: your business name as lettering (uploaded image)
- **Up to five brand colors**
- **Services** you offer

## Create a brand

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Brands">
    Click **Brands** in the sidebar, then **Add brand**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basics">
    Enter the brand name and pick an industry. These are the only required
    fields. Add a tagline, website, logo, and colors if you have them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create brand**. It is available to every tool immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The brand overview

Open any brand card to see its overview. The banner at the top scores how complete the profile is out of 100, and **Complete profile** takes you to the next missing detail.

- **Identity.** Type the business name, industry, service area, tagline and About straight into their fields. A **Save changes** button appears once you change something. Point at the logo to see **Change logo**; click it to **Upload new**, **Adjust crop** or **Remove logo**. The wordmark uploads in the **Edit** window.
- **Brand voice & positioning.** Tone, phrasing rules, price positioning and what ads should ask for. These save on their own as you pick or leave a field.
- **Audience & proof.** Who you sell to, what sets you apart, and the proof behind it.
- **Design language.** Your color swatches and typography samples.
- **Links.** Your website and social profiles. For Instagram, X, TikTok and YouTube the start of the web address (for example `youtube.com/@`) is already shown, so you type only your handle. For your website, Facebook and LinkedIn, type the address; `https://` is already there.
- **Services** as a list of pills. Click the plus pill, type a service, and press Enter. Click the × on a pill to remove it.
- **Integrations.** A connected tool's **Connected** button opens **Disconnect**, which ends that connection for this brand; press it twice to confirm.

## How the AI uses your brand

When you generate ads through the [Generator](/create-ads/ad-generator), [Copier](/create-ads/ad-copier), or [AI Agent](/ai-agent/overview), Raddivus reads your brand profile automatically. The agent already knows your name, industry, services, and colors before you type anything, so it never asks basic questions about your business. Your **About** goes into ad writing and strategy, and your **wordmark** is attached to image ads so they use your own lettering wherever your name appears. For deeper context that shapes the AI's writing voice, add material to the brand's [knowledge base](/brands/knowledge-base).

## Edit or delete

Click **Edit** in the Identity section to change anything, including the logo, wordmark and colors. To delete, click **Delete** beside it (or use the menu on the brand card) and confirm. Deleting a brand also removes its knowledge base and any folders linked to it, so it cannot be undone.
