Send a new client one private link instead of a questionnaire, a call and a chase for passwords.

**Where:** open the brand → **Client onboarding** tab. **Cost:** free, except the optional website research (2 credits). **Plans:** Professional and VIP. Everyone on your account sees the same onboarding.

## Before your first one

Open **Settings → Client onboarding** and fill in what every onboarding starts from: your agency name, your Meta Business ID, Google Ads manager ID and Google email, your booking link, a welcome message, what happens next, a reply-to email, and (optionally) a note and a how-to video for each access step. The whole team shares these.

## Send the link

1. On the brand's **Client onboarding** tab, start an onboarding and enter the client's name and email.
2. Choose which questions to ask and which ad and tracking accounts to request access to. Defaults are picked for the brand's industry.
3. Optional: **Research first** reads up to 30 pages of the client's website and pre-fills what it finds (business details, services, offers, the Meta pixel and Google tags on the site), each with the page it came from. You edit or clear anything before sending; nothing reaches the client until you do. It costs 2 credits and is refunded in full if it finds nothing usable.
4. Send it by email, or copy the link to send yourself (WhatsApp included).

**Resend link** sends a fresh one and turns every earlier link off. **Revoke** turns the onboarding off. The account owner or an admin can delete the onboarding with everything in it.

## What your client sees

No account and no password. On their phone or computer they:

- meet your team and confirm the business details,
- grant access to each account you asked for, with steps written for that platform. A step they cannot do themselves can be **forwarded** to the right person, who gets a link to that one step only,
- book the kickoff call,
- then, if you switched it on, answer questions about their services, area, offers, competitors and past marketing (only the ones that fit their industry) and upload photos, videos and PDFs.

Every answer saves as it is given, so they can stop and continue later on another device. Every question can be skipped; skipped ones stay on their list of things to do. If an answer is too vague to use in an ad, one short question appears under it asking for the missing detail.

## What you see

The tab shows progress, a launch readiness score, the client's files and a live activity feed.

- When the client says an access step is done, any teammate checks it and clicks **Confirm access**.
- When the client finishes a part, Raddivus tidies their written answers into short facts and drafts a follow-up question for anything still vague. Edit a follow-up and send it, or dismiss it. A sent one appears on the client's list.
- **Apply to brand** shows what the answers would change on the brand, each with a tick (nothing the brand already has is ticked for you), and saves a **Client brief** to the brand's Knowledge Base. The AI Agent reads it.

## Reminders and alerts

If the client stalls, Raddivus emails them a reminder 1, 3 and 5 days after you sent the link, while something required is still open: access steps, business details, the kickoff call, or a follow-up you sent. Each reminder lists what is left and opens the most important step. The optional second part never triggers a reminder.

Every reminder has a **Stop these reminders** link. You can also pause reminders from the tab's menu. They stop on their own once the onboarding is completed or revoked.

Your team is told in the bell when:

- the client finishes a part,
- follow-up questions are ready to review (one notification for all of them),
- three reminders went out with nothing done. A task "Call {client}: onboarding stalled" also appears on the brand's **Tasks**.

## Limits

- One active onboarding per brand.
- 100 files and 2 GB per onboarding, 200 MB per file. Photos, videos and PDFs only; a file that is not really one of those is refused.
- A link works until 30 days after the onboarding is completed, and never longer than 90 days after it was sent.

## Privacy

Anyone holding the link can open it, so the email asks your client not to share it. A wrong, expired or revoked link shows one plain page that names nobody. Client pages are hidden from search engines. Files are stored privately and only your teammates can see them. Deleting the onboarding deletes its answers, files and links; what you already applied to the brand stays on the brand.
