Every brand has its own knowledge base, a place to keep the source material the AI uses to write on-voice content. It is **free** on all plans.

## Which tools read it

Every AI tool reads your knowledge base, at no extra credit cost:

- **Ad Generator** and **Ad Copier**: when a brand is selected, they look up the passages that match your offer and instructions and use them in the ad.
- **Publish to Meta Ads**: the headline, primary text and button are written from the passages that match the ad.
- **Content Studio** and the **Topic radar** read it when they plan.
- **Instagram Content** grounds every post in it.
- **The AI Agent** can read it too. Type `@` in the chat box to point it at a document or folder.

Everyone on your team shares the same knowledge base for a brand. A brand with an empty knowledge base works exactly as before.

## Open the knowledge base

Click any brand card to open it, then switch to the **Knowledge Base** tab.

## What you can store

- **Editable documents** in a Notion-style editor. Write FAQs, your brand story, positioning, service descriptions, tone guides, and offer details. Use headings, lists, quotes, code blocks, dividers, tables, links and pictures.
- **File uploads.** Markdown (`.md`), plain text (`.txt`), and Word (`.docx`) files are converted to editable documents automatically. Images and PDFs are stored as assets.

## Space

Your account has one pool of knowledge base space, shared by every brand and every teammate:

| Plan | Space |
| --- | --- |
| Starter (and the free trial) | 5 GB |
| Professional | 25 GB |
| VIP | 100 GB |

- Uploaded files, pictures inside documents and written text all count. The PDF previews Raddivus makes do not.
- There is no limit on how many items a brand holds, only on space. Each file can be up to **25 MB**.
- When the space is full, new documents, uploads and imports are refused. You can still open, edit, download and delete everything, and editing a document you already have always works.
- Moving to a smaller plan never deletes anything. You keep every item and can add new ones once you are back under your space.
- To get more space, move to a bigger plan.

## The list

Your documents and files show as a list or a grid (the switch is at the top). The list has three columns: **Name**, **Uploaded By** and **Uploaded On**. **Sort** orders it by **Newest first**, **Oldest first** or **Name, A to Z**. A long list is split into pages: pick 5, 10, 20, 25 or 100 rows per page (10 to start) and move between pages at the bottom, where "1–10 of 14" says which items you are seeing out of how many. At the top right of the list, a thin bar shows how much of your account's knowledge base space is filled, with the words under it, for example "1.2 GB used · 23.8 GB left · shared by every brand". The bar turns amber when the space is almost full and red when it is full. Hover over the dotted words, or tap them on a phone, to see your plan's total.

## Folders

The list beside your documents shows **All** and every folder, sub-folders indented under their folder. **New folder** at the bottom makes one. The **+** on a folder (shown when you point at it, or always on a touch screen) opens **New folder**, **Upload files…** and **Upload folder…**, each adding straight into that folder.

A folder you make is saved for the whole team: it stays when it is empty, after a reload and for everyone. It goes only when someone deletes it from its **…** menu, where **Rename** is too. Folders are free and take no space.

## Add a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create document">
    On the Knowledge Base tab, click **+ Document**. The **Create document**
    page offers **Blank**, **Upload** (files or a whole folder) and **Notion**,
    and 12 templates to start from: Brand voice, Services and pricing, Ad copy
    rules, Claims and compliance, Offers and discounts, Customer personas,
    FAQs, Competitors, Reviews to quote, Seasonal calendar, Photo guidelines
    and Service area. Templates are free on every plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write">
    Use the editor to add your content. Type `/` on an empty line to add a
    block: text, headings, lists, a quote, a code block, a divider, an image or
    a table. It saves on its own a moment after you stop typing: the note on
    the **Last updated** row reads **Saving…** as you type, then **Saved**. If
    a save fails, the note says so and offers **Retry**. **Created on** and
    **Last updated** read as "Just now", "Yesterday" or "3 days ago"; hover
    the words for the exact date and time in your time zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate">
    On wide screens, short lines down the left edge mark each heading. Hover
    them to see the headings, and click one to jump to that section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Under the title you see who created the document, when (in your time zone),
and which folder it is in; click the folder name to move it. Pressing Enter or
the down arrow in the title jumps to the start of the text. The **…** menu at
the top right offers **Duplicate** (the copy opens), **Move to**,
**Download** (a Markdown `.md` file, free for anyone who can see the
document; pictures are left out) and **Delete**.

To remove a picture or a divider line, point at it (or tab to it) and click
the small ×: it turns into a red **Delete**, and a second click removes it.
On a phone or tablet the × is always showing.

**Auto-fill**, beside **+ Document**, is coming soon.

## Add a picture to a document

Type `/` and choose **Image**, or paste or drop a picture into the document.
JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF pictures up to **10 MB** work; large pictures are
shrunk in your browser before they upload. Pictures are stored privately with
the brand's knowledge files, so only people on your account can open them.
They are free, count toward your knowledge base space, and are deleted with
the document.

## Mention a task or another document

Type `@` in a document to link one of the brand's tasks or another knowledge base document (uploaded files included). A menu opens at your cursor with **Tasks** and **Knowledge base**; type to narrow it, then pick with the arrow keys and Enter, or click. Mentions are free on every plan.

- The pick becomes a small chip, the same look as a mention in a task's brief: a task wears its status mark, a document the mark of its file type. Click a chip to open the task or document, even while you are writing.
- The chip always shows the current name. Rename the task and every document mentioning it shows the new name.
- Everyone reads a mention as they are allowed to see it. A task that was deleted, or that sits in a project you are not on, shows as **Unavailable**, never its name.
- The menu only offers what you can see, and a document never offers itself. When you edit a document, the mentions a teammate added stay, even ones you cannot open.
- **Download** keeps each mention as a link with its name. **Duplicate** keeps the mentions you can see. On a grid card a mention reads "@task" or "@document".
- The AI Agent and a connected AI assistant see each mention with its current name and can open the task or document behind it, only when you are allowed to see it.

## Upload files or a folder

On **Create document**, click **Upload** and pick **Files…**, or drag files onto the Knowledge Base. Markdown, text, and Word files become editable documents. Images and PDFs are kept as assets. Open an item's **…** menu to rename, move or delete it.

To bring in a whole folder, drag it in, or click **Upload** and pick **Folder…**. It arrives inside the folder you have open, with all its sub-folders and the same names.

- Files the knowledge base does not take, files over 25 MB, and files nested more than 5 folders deep are skipped, each listed in the upload panel with the reason. The rest still upload.
- Hidden system files (such as `.DS_Store`) are ignored.
- If the folder is bigger than the space you have left, nothing is uploaded and the message says how big the upload is and how much space is left, so a folder never arrives half-copied. Picking several loose files works the same way.
- On a phone, **Upload** picks files only, because phones cannot pick a folder.

### The upload panel

Every upload opens a panel in the bottom-right corner (a bar across the bottom on a phone). It stays while you move to other pages, so you can keep working. Three files go at a time.

- **Each file** still on the move shows its name and, under it, Waiting or Uploading…. A file that failed says why, with **Retry** to send it again; a skipped file says why it was left out.
- **Finished files** fold into one line ("12 uploaded"). Click **Show** to list them, and click one to open it.
- **The header** says how far it has got ("Uploading 7 of 23") with a thin bar of files finished, and at the end how it went ("21 uploaded, 2 failed"). With two or more failed, the circular arrow retries them all.
- **Cancel one file:** point at its row and click the **×** (always showing on a phone or tablet). It works on a file still waiting and on one already sending. A file cancelled mid-send is removed as soon as it lands, so it never appears in your Knowledge Base.
- **Cancel all:** the **×** at the top right stops every file still waiting or sending. Once everything has finished, the same **×** closes the panel.
- Closing the browser tab while files are uploading asks first.

## Act on several items at once

Tick the box at the start of a row (list view) or in the top corner of a card (grid view). Shift-click ticks everything between two items, and the box in the list's header ticks or clears the whole folder.

A bar appears at the bottom with **Select all**, **Move to…** and **Delete**. **Move to…** opens a list of folders right above the bar. Delete asks first and names the items. If some items could not be moved or deleted, the message names them and the rest are done.

- Ticks stay when you switch between list and grid, and clear when you open another folder or press Escape.
- Items still importing from Notion cannot be ticked.

## Who can edit a brand's knowledge base

Everyone on your team can read every brand's knowledge base. The account owner and admins can add, change, move and delete items in every brand, including items a teammate added.

A member can edit only the brands you pick for them:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the member">
    Go to **Settings > Team** and open the **…** menu on the member's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the brands">
    Choose **Knowledge base editing**, tick the brands they may edit, and click
    **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- A member without access can read the brand's knowledge base and open its documents, but sees no buttons to add, upload, rename, move or delete. To edit, they ask the account owner or an admin.
- Removing someone from the team removes their picks.
- Deleting an item never removes a picture that another document of the brand still shows.

## Import from Notion

If your FAQs, positioning or service descriptions already live in Notion, import them instead of retyping. It is **free** on all plans.

Open **Settings > Integrations > Notion**, or click **+ Document** on a brand's Knowledge Base tab and pick **Notion** (it opens the same screen with that brand already chosen).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Notion">
    You are taken to Notion, which asks which pages Raddivus may read. Raddivus
    can only see the pages you choose there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the pages">
    The pages you shared appear as a list that keeps its nesting. Ticking a page
    ticks its sub-pages too. Pages already in this brand are marked "Already
    here."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a folder">
    Pick an existing folder, the top level, or a new folder, and rename any
    document before it is created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    The documents appear in that folder right away and fill in as they finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What comes across:** headings, paragraphs, lists, to-dos, toggles, callouts, quotes, code blocks, dividers, tables, and text formatting. Images, video, files, embeds and databases are not imported yet; Raddivus tells you when something in a page was skipped.

**Good to know:**

- Importing the same page again refreshes its content and keeps any name or folder you changed.
- Imports count toward your account's knowledge base space. When the space is full, the picker says so, and only pages you already imported can be refreshed.
- Raddivus only reads from Notion. It never writes anything back.
- Your Notion connection is yours. Your whole team can read the imported documents, but a teammate who wants to refresh one connects their own Notion first.
- Disconnecting Notion in Settings keeps the documents you already imported.
- Notion shows as **Connected** on a brand only after you tick that brand under **Brands using this connection** on the Notion screen in Settings. See [ClickUp and Notion are linked per brand](/connections/connect-clickup#clickup-and-notion-are-linked-per-brand).

<Tip>
  The richer your knowledge base, the more your generated content sounds like
  you. A brand with no documents still works, but adding a few pages noticeably
  improves relevance.
</Tip>
