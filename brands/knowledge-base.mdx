Every brand has its own knowledge base, a place to keep the source material the AI uses to write on-voice content. It is **free** on all plans.

## Which tools read it

Every AI tool reads your knowledge base, at no extra credit cost:

- **Ad Generator** and **Ad Copier**: when a brand is selected, they look up the passages that match your offer and instructions and use them in the ad.
- **Publish to Meta Ads**: the headline, primary text and button are written from the passages that match the ad.
- **Content Studio** and the **Topic radar** read it when they plan.
- **Instagram Content** grounds every post in it.
- **The AI Agent** can read it too. Type `@` in the chat box to point it at a document or folder.

Everyone on your team shares the same knowledge base for a brand. A brand with an empty knowledge base works exactly as before.

## Open the knowledge base

Click any brand card to open it, then switch to the **Knowledge Base** tab.

## What you can store

- **Editable documents** in a Notion-style editor. Write FAQs, your brand story, positioning, service descriptions, tone guides, and offer details. Use headings, lists, quotes, code blocks, dividers, tables, links and pictures.
- **File uploads.** Markdown (`.md`), plain text (`.txt`), and Word (`.docx`) files are converted to editable documents automatically. Images and PDFs are stored as assets.

## Limits

These are the same on every plan:

- Up to **20 items** per brand
- Up to **25 MB** per file

## Add a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="New document">
    On the Knowledge Base tab, click **New document** and give it a title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write">
    Use the editor to add your content. Type `/` on an empty line to add a
    block: text, headings, lists, a quote, a code block, a divider, an image or
    a table. It saves on its own a moment after you stop typing: the note on
    the **Last updated** row reads **Saving…** as you type, then **Saved**. If
    a save fails, the note says so and offers **Retry**. **Created on** and
    **Last updated** read as "Just now", "Yesterday" or "3 days ago"; hover
    the words for the exact date and time in your time zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate">
    On wide screens, short lines down the left edge mark each heading. Hover
    them to see the headings, and click one to jump to that section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Under the title you see who created the document, when (in your time zone),
and which folder it is in. The **…** menu at the top right duplicates or
deletes the document.

## Add a picture to a document

Type `/` and choose **Image**, or paste or drop a picture into the document.
JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF pictures up to **10 MB** work; large pictures are
shrunk in your browser before they upload. Pictures are stored privately with
the brand's knowledge files, so only people on your account can open them.
They are free, do not count toward the 20-item limit, and are deleted with the
document.

## Upload files or a folder

Click **Upload** and pick **Files…**, or drag files in. Markdown, text, and Word files become editable documents. Images and PDFs are kept as assets. Open an item's **…** menu to rename, move or delete it.

To bring in a whole folder, drag it in, or click **Upload** and pick **Folder…**. It arrives inside the folder you have open, with all its sub-folders and the same names. While it uploads, the count under the heading reads "Uploading 3 of 12…".

- Files the knowledge base does not take, files over 25 MB, and files nested more than 5 folders deep are named in one message and skipped. The rest still upload.
- Hidden system files (such as `.DS_Store`) are ignored.
- If the folder holds more files than the brand has room for, nothing is uploaded and the message says how many items of room are left, so a folder never arrives half-copied. Picking several loose files works the same way.
- On a phone, **Upload** picks files only, because phones cannot pick a folder.

## Act on several items at once

Tick the box at the start of a row (list view) or in the top corner of a card (grid view). Shift-click ticks everything between two items, and the box in the list's header ticks or clears the whole folder.

A bar appears at the bottom with **Select all**, **Move to…** and **Delete**. Delete asks first and names the items. If some items could not be moved or deleted, the message names them and the rest are done.

- Ticks stay when you switch between list and grid, and clear when you open another folder or press Escape.
- Items still importing from Notion cannot be ticked.
- Team members cannot move or delete; owners and admins can.

## Import from Notion

If your FAQs, positioning or service descriptions already live in Notion, import them instead of retyping. It is **free** on all plans.

Open **Settings > Integrations > Notion**, or click **Import from Notion** on a brand's Knowledge Base tab (it opens the same screen with that brand already chosen).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Notion">
    You are taken to Notion, which asks which pages Raddivus may read. Raddivus
    can only see the pages you choose there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the pages">
    The pages you shared appear as a list that keeps its nesting. Ticking a page
    ticks its sub-pages too. Pages already in this brand are marked "Already
    here."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a folder">
    Pick an existing folder, the top level, or a new folder, and rename any
    document before it is created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    The documents appear in that folder right away and fill in as they finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What comes across:** headings, paragraphs, lists, to-dos, toggles, callouts, quotes, code blocks, dividers, tables, and text formatting. Images, video, files, embeds and databases are not imported yet; Raddivus tells you when something in a page was skipped.

**Good to know:**

- Importing the same page again refreshes its content and keeps any name or folder you changed.
- Imports count toward the 20-item limit. The picker shows how many slots are left.
- Raddivus only reads from Notion. It never writes anything back.
- Your Notion connection is yours. Your whole team can read the imported documents, but a teammate who wants to refresh one connects their own Notion first.
- Disconnecting Notion in Settings keeps the documents you already imported.

<Tip>
  The richer your knowledge base, the more your generated content sounds like
  you. A brand with no documents still works, but adding a few pages noticeably
  improves relevance.
</Tip>
