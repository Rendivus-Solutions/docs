The community feed is a shared space where Raddivus users post questions, tips, and results. It is **free** on all plans.

## What you can do

- **Browse** the feed and sort by recent, most liked, or most commented.
- **Filter by tag** to find questions, tips, or showcases.
- **Post** your own update with text, up to four images, a video link, or a poll.
- **React** with likes, save posts privately, and comment.
- **Vote** in polls and see live results.

## Create a post

Use the composer at the top of the feed. Add a title, your content, and optional tags and media, then post. You can edit or delete your own posts from the menu on the post.

<Tip>
  Share a win or ask a question. The feed is a fast way to learn what is working
  for other small businesses running ads.
</Tip>
