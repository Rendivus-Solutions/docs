title: "Guests"
description: "Invite a freelancer or contractor to work on the tasks you assign them, without using a team seat. Professional and VIP, 2 guests per person on your team, free."
---

A guest is someone outside your team, such as a freelance designer, who works only on the tasks you give them. A guest does not use a team seat and never sees your brands, ads, knowledge base or anything else in your workspace.

**Where:** sidebar → **Settings → Team**. **Cost:** free (0 credits). **Plans:** Professional and VIP. On Starter the Guest role is shown locked.

## How many guests you can have

Each person on your team, you included, brings **2 guest spots**. A team of 2 can have up to 4 guests. A pending guest invitation holds a spot until it is accepted, declined, revoked or expires.

## Invite a guest

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team settings">Go to **Settings → Team**. Only the owner and admins can invite.</Step>
  <Step title="Pick the Guest role">
    Enter the person's email address and choose **Guest** as the role. The
    form shows how many guest spots you have left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept">
    The invitation email says it is guest access to the tasks you assign. Once
    they accept, they appear in the guest list in **Settings → Team**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Someone who is already a member of your team cannot also be your guest, and the other way round: remove them first, then invite them in the other role.

## Give a guest work

Assign a task to a guest the same way you assign a teammate: open the task and pick them in the people picker, where they are marked **Guest**. They get an email and a notification with a link to the task.

A guest cannot be @-mentioned, sent a task through the share card, or added to a project.

## What a guest sees

A guest works on their own simple pages, without the Raddivus sidebar:

- **Guest tasks**: every task assigned to them, grouped by team if they help more than one.
- **A task**: its title, team, brand and due date, the brief and its pictures, the sub-tasks and the comments.

| A guest can | A guest cannot |
| --- | --- |
| Move the status (To do, In progress, In review, Done) | Change the title, brief, due date or people |
| Add comments and pictures to a comment | Edit or delete comments, or remove or download pictures |
| Read the brief and sub-tasks | See brands, ads, the knowledge base or other tasks |

Guests never spend your credits.

<Note>
  If a guest has their own Raddivus account and plan, they keep using their own
  workspace as normal, and a **Guest tasks** row appears in their sidebar. A
  guest without a plan of their own goes straight to their guest tasks when they
  sign in.
</Note>

## Remove a guest

In **Settings → Team**, open the guest's **…** menu and choose **Remove guest**. They are taken off every task right away and lose access at once. Their comments stay on the tasks.
