Saved Ads organizes your generated ads into folders that you link to your brands.

**Where:** sidebar → **Saved Ads**. **Cost:** free. **Plans:** all.

## Create a folder

Go to **Saved Ads** in the sidebar, click **New folder**, name it, and optionally link it to a brand for easy filtering and color.

## Save ads

From any Copier or Generator results screen, select an ad and use the **Save** action to add it to a folder. The AI Agent can also save the ads it generates directly to your folders.

## Manage saved ads

- Browse folders from the sidebar or the Saved Ads section.
- Hover any saved ad to download or delete it.
- Enable multi-select to choose several ads at once.
- Bulk download selected ads as a **ZIP** file.
- Rename or delete entire folders. Deleting a folder removes the ads inside it.

## Ask the AI Agent about a folder or one ad

In the chat box (on Home or the Agent page), open the **+** menu and choose **Mention folder**. Click a folder to mention the whole folder, or hover it (tap it on a phone) to see its ads, and pick just one. Each ad shows a small picture, its headline or ad type, and a line that tells similar ads apart; hover an ad to see its picture large. Then ask, for example "why does this one work?", "what would you change in the picture?" or "write three variations of this". The agent reads the ad's copy (headline, text, call to action and ad type) and looks at its picture, for up to 4 ads per message. Mentioning a whole folder gives it the words only. The picture has no separate price: it counts toward that message's usual Agent credits.

On a team account every teammate sees and can save into every folder. Renaming, changing the brand, deleting a folder or removing an ad is limited to whoever created it and to the team owner and admins. A folder a teammate created says so under its name.
