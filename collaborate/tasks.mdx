Every brand has its own task list, shared with everyone on your account.

**Where:** sidebar → **Tasks**, for the brand you have open (switch brands at the top of the sidebar). **Cost:** free, no credits. **Plans:** all.

## What a task holds

- **Status:** Backlog, Todo, In progress, In review, Done, Cancelled or Duplicate. Set a task to Duplicate and pick the original: the row then says "Duplicate of" with a link to it.
- **Priority**, the **platform** it is for (Meta, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Google Ads, LinkedIn, your website or other), the **people** on it (up to 10) and a **due date**, with an optional time shown on each teammate's own clock. A task turns red the moment its time passes.
- A **blocked reason**, when something is in the way. It shows on the row until someone clears it.
- A **brief**, **comments**, **attachments** and a feed of everything that changed.
- **Sub-tasks**, one level deep. Open a task and click **Add sub-task**: type a title, set its status, platform, people and due date right there, press Enter to add the next one. The parent shows how many are finished ("2/5").
- A **repeat** rule: pick "Set repeat…" in the due date menu. The task comes back when someone moves it to the status you chose (Done by default), either as the same task with its next due date or as a fresh copy carrying what you tick. Nothing runs on a timer.

## List or board

The switch next to **New task** turns the list into a **board** with one column per status. Drag a card to another column to change its status (long press on a phone), or use the status menu on the card. The board is saved in the page address, so a shared link opens it the same way.

To change many tasks at once, tick the box at the start of each row (Shift+click ticks a range, Ctrl or Cmd+click adds one, Esc clears). A bar appears at the bottom with Status, Priority, Assign, Assign to me, Due date, Sub-task of…, Copy links and Delete.

## Filters and saved views

Filter by status, priority, person, platform and due date, including any day or range you pick on a calendar.

Keep a set of filters you use often as a **saved view**:

1. Set the filters (for example Assignee = Me and Due = Overdue).
2. Click **Save view** at the end of the filter row and give it a name.
3. The view appears as a tab above the filters, next to **All tasks**. Opening it puts back its filters, sort and list or board layout.

Change the filters while a view is open and **Save** updates it, or **Save as new view** keeps both. A view lives on the brand where you saved it and starts private to you. Its **…** menu renames it, deletes it, or turns on **Share with your account** so everyone on the account sees it too. Only the person who saved a view, or the account owner, can change or delete it.

## Open a task

Click anywhere on a task's row (or its card) and the task slides in from the right, over the list. The **×** at the top (or Escape) closes it and leaves the list exactly as you left it, filters included. The **…** next to it copies a link to the task or deletes it.

### As a full page

The expand button beside the **…** opens the task as a **full page** instead. The list waits behind, unchanged, and comes back when you press × or **Tasks** in the breadcrumb. The panel button in the same spot brings the side panel back.

- On the full page the details (status, priority, people, due date, platform and the rest) sit in a column on the right.
- The top right shows where the task sits in the list you came from ("2 / 18"), with up and down arrows that open the previous or next task without going back, in the same order and with the same filters as the list.
- Raddivus remembers which way you used last on this browser, so the next task you click opens the same way. On a phone a task always fills the screen.
- A link to a task open as a full page opens it as a full page for whoever follows it.

### Activity

Comments and changes sit together under **Activity**, newest first. Each change says what it changed to ("set priority to High", "assigned Aadrika Singhal"), with a small glyph that says what kind of change it was. The five newest show at once; **Show N more** reveals the rest.

The comment box sits at the foot of the task. Post with the yellow arrow or Ctrl or Cmd+Enter. Your own comments can be edited or deleted from the **…** on the comment.

## Mentions

In a comment or the brief, type **@** (or press the **@** button in the comment box) to mention:

- a **teammate** on your account,
- another **task** on the brand,
- a **Knowledge Base** document or folder,
- one of your **saved-ads** folders.

A menu opens at your cursor, grouped by People, Tasks, Knowledge base and Saved ads, and narrows as you type. Pick with Enter or a click and the mention becomes a small blue chip. In a posted comment or a saved brief, clicking a task, document or folder chip opens it; a person's chip just names them. A chip keeps the name it had when it was inserted, even if the task or document is renamed later, and still opens the right one.

A mentioned teammate hears about it in two places:

- **The bell** in the app: "Gabriel mentioned you in a comment on Launch post", which opens the task.
- **Email**, with the first few lines of the comment and a link to the task.

Mentioning yourself sends nothing. Editing a comment only tells someone newly added to it. Mentions only ever point at people on your account and things on the same brand.

## Pictures

The brief, comments and the New task window take pictures (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB each; large pictures are shrunk before they are sent, and photo location data is removed). How you add one decides where it goes:

- **Inside the text:** paste a screenshot (Ctrl or Cmd+V), drop an image in, or type **/** and pick **Add image**. A thin blue line shows where a dragged picture will land.
- **As an attachment:** click the **upload arrow**. On a task, attachments are listed under **Attachments** (after Subtasks), each as a row with a small preview, the file name and its size. On a comment, the picture is listed the same way under the comment.

Click any picture to see it full size and step through the rest. Under the full-size picture, **Download** saves it under the name it was added with, and **Delete** turns red on the first click and removes it on the second. You can also hover over a picture (or tab to it) and click the **×** in its corner or at the end of its row; it asks once before deleting. On a phone or tablet the × is always showing. Whoever added a picture, or the account owner, can remove it.

A comment can be only a picture, with no words.

## A draft is never lost

Once you have typed or picked anything in the New task window, closing it asks first: Cancel, the ×, a click outside the window or Escape open a small "Discard this task?" box, where **Keep editing** takes you back and only **Discard draft** throws the draft away. An empty window still closes in one click.

## When someone gives you a task

When a teammate adds you to a task, you are told in two places:

- **The bell** in the app: "Gabriel assigned you a task", which opens the task.
- **Email**, with each task's title, priority, due date, the first few lines of its brief and a link to it. Several tasks assigned to you at once arrive as one email listing them all.

Assigning yourself sends nothing, and the people already on a task hear nothing more when someone else joins. The email never carries the whole brief: the rest stays in Raddivus, behind the link.

## Who sees what

Everyone on your account sees and can work on every task of a brand they can open. Comments can be edited or deleted only by whoever wrote them or the account owner. When a teammate leaves, their tasks stay and they are taken off them, their private saved views are deleted and their shared ones pass to the account owner.
