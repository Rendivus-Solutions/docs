Every brand has its own task list, shared with everyone on your account.

**Where:** sidebar → **Tasks**, for the brand you have open (switch brands at the top of the sidebar). **Cost:** free, no credits. **Plans:** all.

## What a task holds

- **Status:** Backlog, Todo, In progress, In review, Done, Cancelled or Duplicate.
- **Priority**, the **platform** it is for (Meta, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Google Ads, LinkedIn, your website or other), **one person** on it and a **due date**, with an optional time shown on each teammate's own clock.
- A **brief**, **comments** and a feed of everything that changed.
- **Sub-tasks**, one level deep. The parent shows how many are finished ("2/5"). Open a task and click **Add sub-task**: the row becomes the new sub-task, where you type its title and can set its status, platform, people and due date (on a phone the last three are behind "…"). Press Enter to add it and start the next one; Escape or × stops.
- A **repeat** rule: pick "Set repeat…" in the due date menu. The task comes back when someone moves it to the status you chose (Done by default), either as the same task with its next due date or as a fresh copy carrying what you tick.

## List or board

The switch next to **New task** turns the list into a **board** with one column per status. Drag a card to another column to change its status (long press on a phone), or use the status menu on the card.

To change many tasks at once, tick the box at the start of each row (Shift+click ticks a range, Esc clears). A bar appears at the bottom with Status, Priority, Assign, Assign to me, Due date, Sub-task of…, Copy links and Delete.

## Filters and saved views

Filter by status, priority, person, platform and due date, including any day or range you pick on a calendar.

Keep a set of filters you use often as a **saved view**:

1. Set the filters (for example Assignee = Me and Due = Overdue).
2. Click **Save view** at the end of the filter row and give it a name.
3. The view appears as a tab above the filters, next to **All tasks**. Opening it puts back its filters, sort and list or board layout.

Change the filters while a view is open and **Save** updates it, or **Save as new view** keeps both. A view lives on the brand where you saved it and starts private to you. Its **…** menu renames it, deletes it, or turns on **Share with your account** so everyone on the account sees it too. Only the person who saved a view, or the account owner, can change or delete it.

## Open a task

Click anywhere on a task's row (or its card on the board) to read the brief, comment and see its history. It opens in a panel beside the list at once, with its title, status, priority, people, due date and platform already showing; the brief, sub-tasks, attachments and activity fill in as they arrive. Shift, Ctrl or Cmd+click on a row ticks it instead of opening it. The **×** at the top right (or Esc) closes it and leaves the list or board exactly as you left it, filters included. The **…** next to it copies a link to the task or deletes it.

Tasks made with Repeat or Make a copy can share a title. Wherever one task points at another (the parent of a sub-task, the original of a duplicate, and the search for either), each task shows its status and its due date, or the day it was created when it has none, so you can tell them apart.

## Pictures

The brief and comments take pictures (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB each). How you add one decides where it goes:

- **Inside the text:** paste a screenshot (Ctrl or Cmd+V), drop an image in, or type **/** and pick **Add image**. A thin blue line shows where a dragged picture will land.
- **As an attachment:** on a task, click the **upload arrow** at the end of the **Attachments** heading, after Subtasks; in a comment, click the **paperclip** in the comment box. Each attached picture is listed as a row with a small preview, the picture's file name and its size (pictures added before names were kept read "Picture").

Click any picture, or its row, to see it full size. Under the full-size picture, **Download** saves it to your computer under the name it was added with, and **Delete** removes it after one small red **Delete** to confirm. You can also delete a picture without opening it: hover over it (or tab to it) and click the **×** in its corner, or at the end of its row, then the small red **Delete**; click anywhere else or press Esc to keep it. On a phone or tablet the × is always showing. Whoever added a picture, or the account owner, can delete it.

A comment can be only a picture, with no words. Ctrl or Cmd+Enter posts.

## When someone gives you a task

When a teammate assigns you a task, you are told in two places:

- **The bell** in the app: "Gabriel assigned you a task", which opens the task.
- **Email**, with each task's title, priority, due date, the first few lines of its brief and a link to it. Several tasks assigned to you at once arrive as one email listing them all.

Assigning yourself sends nothing. The email never carries the whole brief: the rest stays in Raddivus, behind the link.

## Who sees what

Everyone on your account sees and can work on every task of a brand they can open. Comments can be edited or deleted only by whoever wrote them or the account owner. When a teammate leaves, their tasks stay and go back to unassigned, their private saved views are deleted and their shared ones pass to the account owner.
