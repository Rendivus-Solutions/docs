title: "Team workspace"
description: "Invite team members, assign roles, and share brands, ads, and folders across your workspace. Available on the VIP plan with 6 seats."
---

Team features let you share your workspace with others. They are available on the **VIP plan**, which includes **6 team seats**.

## Roles

| Role       | Brand access         | Ad tools    | Team management              |
| ---------- | -------------------- | ----------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Owner**  | Create, edit, delete | Full access | Invite, remove, change roles |
| **Admin**  | Create, edit, delete | Full access | Invite and remove members    |
| **Member** | View only            | Full access | View members, leave team     |

## Invite team members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team settings">Go to **Settings → Team**.</Step>
  <Step title="Send an invite">
    Enter the email address of the person you want to invite and choose their
    role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    Copy the invite link and send it. The invitee clicks it to join
    automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Invite links expire after 7 days. You can send as many invitations as you like, as long as you have available seats.

## What's shared

All team members share access to brand profiles, saved ads and folders, and generated ad history.

<Note>
  Agent conversations are private. Team members cannot see each other's chats.
</Note>
