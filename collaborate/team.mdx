Team features let you share your workspace with others. You share brands, ads, and folders with teammates while your AI Agent conversations stay private.

**Where:** sidebar → **Settings → Team**. **Cost:** free. **Plans:** VIP only (3 seats). Starter and Professional do not include team seats.

## Roles

| Role       | Brand access         | Ad tools    | Team management              |
| ---------- | -------------------- | ----------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Owner**  | Create, edit, delete | Full access | Invite, remove, change roles |
| **Admin**  | Create, edit, delete | Full access | Invite and remove members    |
| **Member** | View only            | Full access | View members, leave team     |

## Invite team members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team settings">Go to **Settings → Team**.</Step>
  <Step title="Send an invite">
    Enter the email address of the person you want to invite and choose their
    role. Sending an invitation does not spend credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track delivery and status">
    Raddivus emails the invitation and notifies existing Raddivus users in the
    app. The invitation tracker shows delivery details and whether each invite
    is pending, accepted, expired, declined, or revoked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage the invitation">
    While an invitation is pending, you can copy its link, resend the email, or
    revoke it. For a closed invitation, send a fresh invitation instead of
    reusing the old link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Invite links expire after 7 days. An outstanding invitation reserves a seat, so
you can send invitations only while the workspace has room within its 3-seat
limit.

## Accept an invitation

Open the invitation from your email or Raddivus notification. If you need to
sign in, create an account, or use Google sign-in, Raddivus keeps the invitation
attached to that flow and returns you to it afterward.

If your profile is incomplete, add a display name and an available username.
You can also add an avatar, bio, location, website, business name, and industry.
After you accept, you join the workspace immediately and skip the standard new-
account onboarding questions.

<Warning>
  Sign in with the email address that received the invitation. For privacy,
  Raddivus shows only a masked address when the signed-in email does not match.
</Warning>

## How team access works

**Every team member inherits the owner's plan.** You don't need your own subscription. Once you accept an invitation, you get full access to all the same tools as the account's VIP plan—no paywall, regardless of your personal plan or trial status.

**The team shares one credit balance.** Credits belong to the workspace account, not to individual members. Everyone spends from the same pool and sees the same remaining balance. The account owner's credit history records which teammate spent what. When a teammate leaves or is removed from the team, they immediately drop back to their own personal plan and balance.

**Shared folders.** On a team account every teammate sees and can save into every folder. Renaming, changing the brand, deleting a folder or removing an ad is limited to whoever created it and to the team owner and admins.

**Billing emails go to the account owner.**

## What's shared

All team members can:
- View and use all workspace brands
- View and use all saved ads and folders
- See all generated ad history
- View and use all connected Meta and Instagram accounts in the ad tools (Ad Analytics, AI Agent, and publishing). **Only the person who connected an account can disconnect it, reconnect it, or change which brand it links to.**

**Private to you:**
- AI Agent conversations—teammates cannot see your chats
- "Memory about you"—your personal notes stay with you

**Shared with the team:**
- Notes about your business and about your clients—only the account owner or an admin can edit or delete these by hand

Leaving a team or being removed ends that workspace access immediately.

<Note>
  Provider credentials and access tokens remain private to the person who
  connected the account.
</Note>
