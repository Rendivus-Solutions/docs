---

Connecting ClickUp lets you send finished Instagram posts straight to your boards as tasks. It is available on **all plans** and costs **no credits**.

## Connect your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Go to **Settings → Connections** and find the **ClickUp** card. Click
    **Connect ClickUp**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    You are taken to ClickUp's login. Sign in if needed and approve the
    integration. This grants Raddivus access to your workspaces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your brands">
    You come back to the ClickUp screen in Settings. Under **Brands using this
    connection**, tick each brand that works in ClickUp. Nothing is ticked for
    you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## ClickUp and Notion are linked per brand

ClickUp shows as **Connected** in the sidebar's integrations menu, on the
Integrations tiles and on a brand's page only for the brands you ticked. A brand
you did not tick shows **Connect**, even while you are signed in to ClickUp.
Notion works the same way, from **Settings → Integrations → Notion**.

- The ticks are shared with your team. Everyone sees which brands are linked,
  and who linked them, and anyone can untick a brand.
- Ticking a brand needs your own ClickUp (or Notion) login. A teammate who has
  not connected sees the list but cannot tick.
- Disconnecting ClickUp or Notion unlinks every brand you linked with it.
- If your login stops working, the brands it is linked to show **Reconnect**.

<Note>ClickUp tokens do not expire, so this is a one-time setup.</Note>

## Sending content to ClickUp

Once a post is finished, open its edit modal and click **Send to ClickUp**. The first time you send to a given list, a one-time **field mapper** appears so you can choose which Raddivus field lands in which ClickUp field. For the full flow, see [Send a post to ClickUp](/instagram/send-to-clickup).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Send to ClickUp button is disabled">
    ClickUp must be connected first, and the post must be finished (text and
    visual done). Connect ClickUp under **Settings → Connections** and try again
    from a completed post.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="ClickUp says Connect on one brand but Connected on another">
    ClickUp is linked per brand. Open **Settings → Connections → ClickUp** and
    tick the brand under **Brands using this connection**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The task was created but the image is missing">
    The task still succeeds even if an attachment fails. Re-send the post to
    update the same task, or check that the image finished composing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
