Connecting Instagram lets you publish and schedule the posts you create in [Instagram content](/instagram/overview). You can connect more than one account and link each to a brand.

## Before you start

<Note>

  - You need an **Instagram Business or Creator** account. Personal accounts are not supported.
  - Your Instagram account must be **linked to a Facebook Page**, because you connect it by signing in with Facebook.
  - Publishing is available on the **Professional** and **VIP** plans. On other plans the account still connects, but publishing is preview-only.
  - There is **no credit cost** to connect or to publish.
  - Publishing to Instagram is rolling out account by account while our Meta app approval completes. If **Post now** or **Schedule** is not yet enabled on your account, contact support and we will switch it on for you.

</Note>

## How the connection works

When you click **Connect Instagram** in **Settings → Connections**, you sign in with **Continue with Facebook** and grant access to the Page linked to your Instagram account. These tokens cannot auto-refresh, so you will occasionally need to reconnect.

<Note>
  Signing in with Instagram directly (no Facebook Page needed) is coming back once
  our Meta app approval completes. Until then, Meta only allows it for a small
  set of test accounts, so it is hidden.
</Note>

## Connect your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Go to **Settings → Connections** and find the **Instagram** card. Click
    **Connect Instagram**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Facebook">
    Click **Continue with Facebook**, sign in, and grant the requested
    permissions for the Page linked to your Instagram account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select accounts and link brands">
    Raddivus shows every Instagram account under that login. Tick the ones you
    want to connect, choose which brand each links to, and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage connected accounts">
    Connected accounts show their username and linked brand, with **Reconnect**
    and **Disconnect** buttons. If a token is close to expiring, a reconnect prompt
    appears. On a team, all teammates can use the connected accounts for publishing,
    but only the person who connected the account can disconnect it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you disconnect an account a brand publishes through and nobody else on the
  team has that brand connected, the brand's scheduled posts are marked as
  needing a reconnect instead of being skipped. The posts are kept, and they go
  out as planned if the account is reconnected in time.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My token expired">
    Open **Settings → Connections** and click **Reconnect Instagram**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see my account in the picker">
    Make sure it is a Business or Creator account, that it is linked to a
    Facebook Page you manage, and that you granted access to that Page during
    sign-in. Personal accounts will not appear.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
