title: "Connect Meta Ads"
description: "Connect your Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Ads account to Raddivus to unlock the AI Agent and Ad Analytics."
---

Connecting a Meta Ads account lets Raddivus read your campaign data and power the [AI Agent](/ai-agent/overview) and [Ad Analytics](/analytics/ad-analytics). Your access token is encrypted and used only to talk to Meta on your behalf.

## Before you start

<Note>

  - You need a **Meta Business account** with at least one ad account (a personal Facebook account is not enough).
  - You need the right role in Meta Business Manager. If you do not have permission for an ad account, Meta will block the connection.

</Note>

## Connect your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Go to **Settings → Connections** and find the **Meta** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Click **Connect Meta Ads**. The tab moves to Meta's secure login, then
    comes back to Raddivus when you are done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and grant access">
    Sign in to Meta and choose which business and ad accounts to share. Approve
    the requested permissions (reading ads, pages, and insights).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the ad accounts">
    Back in Raddivus, a window lists every ad account your login can reach,
    each with its business picture, ID, currency and status. Active accounts
    come first; disabled or closed ones are listed after them and cannot be
    picked. Search by name, ID or business (your ticks stay while you search),
    and the line under the search shows how many slots your plan has left.
    **Select all** ticks everything shown when it fits your slots, and **Clear**
    unticks everything. Press **Connect** or Enter in the search box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link to a brand">
    Back in Raddivus, each connected ad account can be linked to one brand.
    Connected accounts appear in a list, each with its business picture, and
    **Reconnect** and **Unlink** options. An account connected before November
    2026 shows its first letter until you reconnect it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How many accounts you can connect

Each plan can keep a limited number of Meta ad accounts connected at once:

- **Starter:** 2 accounts
- **Professional:** 10 accounts
- **VIP:** Unlimited
- **Free trial:** 2 accounts

The limit is per workspace account, not per brand. Re-connecting an account you already have does not use a slot. Disconnecting an ad account immediately frees a slot. If you have more accounts connected than your current plan allows (perhaps because you downgraded or the limits were recently introduced), everything stays connected and usable. You just cannot add a new account until you upgrade or disconnect something.

## Where it's used

- **[AI Agent](/ai-agent/overview)** reads and analyzes the connected account and can propose changes for your approval.
- **[Ad Analytics](/analytics/ad-analytics)** shows a live gallery of every ad with its metrics.

## Disconnecting an account

Disconnecting one ad account does not disconnect the others. Facebook Login for Business grants one permission per business portfolio, and that permission can cover multiple ad accounts at once. Raddivus only revokes the permission when the account you are disconnecting is the last one using it. Everything else stays connected.

## Safety

When you upload a generated ad to Meta through the agent, it is always created in **paused** status, so it never spends budget until you activate it yourself in Meta Ads Manager.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The agent says it can't access my account">
    Your Meta token may have expired or been revoked. Go to **Settings →
    Connections** and click **Reconnect** on the account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Connection fails with a permissions error">
    Ad account access is controlled by Meta Business Manager. Confirm you have
    the right role on the ad account, then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An ad account is missing or cannot be ticked">
    The list shows the ad accounts your Meta login can reach. If one is missing,
    check your role on it in Meta Business Manager and connect again. An account
    that is greyed out is not active on Meta (disabled, closed, unsettled or
    under review) and cannot run ads; fix it in Meta first. If every remaining
    row is locked, your plan's slots are used: press **Upgrade** under the list,
    or disconnect an account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
