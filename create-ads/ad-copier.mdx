The Ad Copier takes an existing ad image, yours or a competitor's, and generates branded variations of it. The AI keeps the layout and style and applies your branding, logo, and offer. No design skills required.

**Where:** sidebar → **Copier**. **Cost:** 1 credit per variation. **Plans:** all.

## Create branded variations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the source ad">
    Drag and drop or paste the ad image you want to base your variations on (up
    to 10 MB).

    {/* SCREENSHOT TODO: the Ad Copier source-ad upload (drag-and-drop) area */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your brand">
    Select your brand or enter your business name and logo. Your logo can be up
    to 2 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your offer (optional)">
    Enter your promotional offer, and optionally upload up to five reference
    images to guide the style.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose format and variations">
    Pick an output format, a resolution (1K, 2K, or 4K), and the number of
    variations (1 to 4).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set toggles (optional)">
    Turn on **Make unique** for more original results, **AI improve** for
    suggested upgrades, and **brand colors** or **brand typography** to apply
    your brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Create unique ad**. Results appear as they finish, usually within 30
    to 120 seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Working with results

Just like the Generator, hover any finished image to select it, then save, download, share, delete, or edit from the selection bar. Selecting, downloading, deleting, saving, and sharing are free.

## Generator or Copier?

| Tool | Use it when you | Starts from |
| ---- | --------------- | ----------- |
| **Ad Copier** | have an ad image you like and want branded versions of that exact style | an existing ad image (yours or a competitor's) |
| **[Ad Generator](/create-ads/ad-generator)** | want original ads built from your offer and brand | your brand and offer, with no source image |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My source image was rejected">
    Use a valid image file (JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF) of 10 MB or less.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The variations look too similar to the source">
    Turn on **Make unique**. Very simple source images may still come out close
    to the original.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
