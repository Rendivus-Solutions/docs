The Image (Ad Generator) creates brand-new ad images from scratch. You provide your offer and brand, choose some creative options, and the AI generates multiple unique ads ready to download or upload to Meta. It does not copy anything, it generates original creative for your business.

**Where:** sidebar → **Image**. **Cost:** 1 credit per variation. **Plans:** all.

## Generate ads

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick your brand">
    Use the brand switcher or the form's brand selector. This fills in your
    business name, industry, logo, and colors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the basics">
    Confirm your business name and industry (required), add a location if
    relevant, and choose an offer such as "Free estimate", "50% off first
    order", or a custom one.

    {/* SCREENSHOT TODO: the Ad Generator generation form showing the offer and brand fields */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add visuals (optional)">
    Upload your logo (up to 2 MB) and up to five reference images (up to 10 MB
    each) to guide the style.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose format and variations">
    Pick an output format, a resolution (1K, 2K, or 4K), and the number of
    variations (1 to 4).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an image model">
    The model chip beside the prompt picks which AI makes the image. The
    default is **Nano Banana 2**, and every model costs the same, so it is
    worth trying a different one when a result is not landing. See
    [Image models](#image-models) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fine-tune (optional)">
    Open **Advanced options** to pick ad types, psychology angles, a tone, and
    an objective. Leave blank to let the AI choose. See the
    [options reference](/create-ads/ad-options-reference) for the full lists.

    {/* SCREENSHOT TODO: the Advanced options panel with ad types, psychology angles, tone, and objective */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate ads**. The button frees up right away and results stream in
    as batches finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Image models

Every account can choose which image model makes the ad. Five are available today:

| Model | Made by | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nano Banana 2** (default) | Google | The everyday pick. Fast, and strongest at keeping your brand's look |
| Nano Banana Pro | Google | Slower and more considered, for when the layout has to be exactly right |
| GPT Image 2 | OpenAI | A different look, when the Google models keep landing in the same place |
| GPT Image 2.5 Flare | OpenAI | Fast and sharp. The one to reach for when you want volume |
| GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst | OpenAI | The premium pick, with tighter control when you edit an existing ad |

<Note>
  Every model costs **1 credit per image on every plan**. Switching models never
  changes the price. The choice applies to that generation (or that edit) only;
  your default stays Nano Banana 2 until you change it in the picker.
</Note>

The [Ad Copier](/create-ads/ad-copier) has no model picker. It uses whichever model Raddivus has set, so Copier results stay consistent for everybody.

## What happens after you click Generate

Generation runs in the background, so you can navigate away or even close the tab. You will see placeholder cards immediately, and finished images replace them as they complete, usually within 30 to 120 seconds depending on how many you requested. If you reopen the page, in-progress batches resume automatically.

<Note>
  The number of images is set by the **Variations** field, not by how many ad
  types or angles you select. Three variations costs 3 credits, regardless of
  the other choices.
</Note>

## Working with results

Hover any finished image and tick its checkbox to select it. Select as many as you like, then use the selection bar to:

{/* SCREENSHOT TODO: the generated results grid with images selected and the selection bar visible */}

- **Save** them to a folder
- **Download** them (one image, or a ZIP for several)
- **Share** them to the Ad Library
- **Delete** them
- **Edit** them with a follow-up prompt

Selecting, downloading, deleting, saving, and sharing are free. An edit re-runs the generator at the normal cost (1 credit per image).

## Advanced canvas view

The Ad Generator also has an **Advanced** canvas mode that shows your brand, style, and generated ads as a visual node graph. Every input, result, and action works the same as the basic view, just laid out spatially.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Generation is slow">
    Large requests take longer. Total images equals your variations count, so
    reducing variations speeds things up. You can leave the page while it runs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My logo or reference image was rejected">
    Use JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF. The logo must be 2 MB or less, and reference
    images 10 MB or less each.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Brand colors aren't showing">
    Make sure the brand color and typography toggles are on and that your brand
    has colors set in its profile.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
