The Generator, Copier, and AI Agent share the same creative options. Leave any selection empty to let the AI choose the best fit for your business and offer.

## Ad types

Fourteen formats are available:

- Meme Ad
- Headline Ad
- Problem-Solution Ad
- What's Inside Ad
- Google Search Style Ad
- Negative Hook Ad
- Testimonial Ad
- Feature-Benefit Ad
- Statistic Ad
- Before & After Ad
- Us vs Them Ad
- Article / News Style Ad
- Apple Notes Style Ad
- Native Feed Ad

## Psychology angles

Ten persuasion angles you can apply to the messaging:

- Curiosity
- Fear / Loss Aversion
- Social Proof
- Authority
- Scarcity
- Urgency
- Emotional Relief
- Skeptic Pattern Break
- Simplicity / Ease
- Price Anchoring

## Tones

Professional, Casual, Aggressive, Conversational, Luxury, Trust-building, Bold / Direct, or let the AI decide.

## Objectives

Lead generation, Website traffic, Conversions, Brand awareness, Retargeting, Appointment booking, or let the AI decide.

## Output formats

| Format        | Ratio  | Best for                      |
| ------------- | ------ | ----------------------------- |
| Feed          | 1:1    | Standard feed placement       |
| Portrait Feed | 4:5    | Feed with more vertical space |
| Story / Reel  | 9:16   | Stories and Reels             |
| Landscape     | 16:9   | Wider placements              |
| Link Ad       | 1.91:1 | Website clicks format         |

## Resolutions

1K, 2K, or 4K. Higher resolutions produce larger, sharper files.
