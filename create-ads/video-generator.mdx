The Video Generator creates short AI-powered video ads. You provide a script and a few choices, and the AI produces a finished clip in the background. It is powered by Kling AI.

**Where:** sidebar → **Video**. **Cost:** 15 credits per video. **Plans:** all.

<img src="/images/concept-video-generator.svg" alt="Illustration of generating a short AI video ad from a script" noZoom />

## Generate a video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick your brand and basics">
    Select your brand, confirm the business name and industry (required), and
    add a location if relevant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the script">
    Describe what the video should show. Be specific about scenes, actions, and
    any dialogue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the format">
    Pick a duration (12, 16, or 20 seconds) and an aspect ratio (16:9, 9:16,
    1:1, or 4:5).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the style and subject">
    Choose a video style (UGC, cinematic, product demo, testimonial, or before &
    after) and subject details. Optionally upload a reference image to guide the
    look.

    {/* SCREENSHOT TODO: the Video Generator script and options form */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate video**. A placeholder card appears while it renders.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after you click Generate

Video generation takes about 2 to 5 minutes and runs in the background. You do not need to wait on the page. When the video is ready, the card updates and you are notified. You can then preview it inline and download the MP4.

<Warning>
  A video costs **15 credits**, much more than an image ad (1 credit). Check
  your balance before you start, since you need at least 15 credits to generate.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It is taking a while">
    Video takes longer than images, typically 2 to 5 minutes. Leave the page if
    you like, the status is saved and you will be notified when it finishes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can only make one at a time">
    Unlike images, video has no variations option. Each request produces one
    video.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
