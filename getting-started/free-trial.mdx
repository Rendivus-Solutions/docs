Every new Raddivus account starts with a free trial, so you can create a brand and generate real ads before you pay for anything.

**Where:** sidebar → **Settings → Billing**. **Cost:** free. **Plans:** new accounts.

## What you get

- A **7-day free trial**, with no credit card required to start.
- **10 trial credits**, granted once. They are not renewable.
- Access to **Professional-level features** during the trial.
- The trial ends after 7 days or once you use the 10 trial credits, whichever comes first.

## What to try first

Ten trial credits is enough to see what Raddivus can do. A good first run:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a brand">
    Add your business name, logo, and colors. Creating brands and building the
    [knowledge base](/brands/knowledge-base) is always free.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate a few ads">
    Use the [Image (Ad Generator)](/create-ads/ad-generator) to create original
    ad images at 1 credit per variation, or the
    [Copier](/create-ads/ad-copier) to make branded versions of an ad you
    already have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft an Instagram post">
    Create a [single post](/instagram/single-post) from your brand knowledge
    base, then edit it for free.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chat with the AI Agent">
    Connect [Meta Ads](/connections/connect-meta) and ask the
    [AI Agent](/ai-agent/overview) about your campaigns. Chatting is free; only
    actions like image generation spend credits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When the trial ends

After the trial you need a paid plan to keep generating. You can still browse the
app, view your saved ads, and manage your brands without a subscription.

## Upgrade to a paid plan

Open **Settings → Billing** to choose Starter, Professional, or VIP, billed monthly
or yearly. Upgrading mid-cycle
grants the difference in credits immediately. See
[Plans and credits](/getting-started/plans-and-credits) to compare plans and
[Credits & billing](/account/credits-and-billing) for the full pricing detail.
