Everything in Raddivus lives inside one dashboard shell. Here is how to move around it.

## The sidebar

The left sidebar is your main navigation. You can collapse it on desktop, and on mobile it opens as a drawer.

- **Home** opens the dashboard with your key metrics and quick actions.
- **Community** opens the shared feed.
- **Tools** groups the creative and analysis tools: Agent, Ad Library, Image (Ad Generator), Copier, Ad Analytics, Video, IG Content, and Saved Ads.
- **Updates** shows what we have shipped and what is planned.
- **Settings** opens your profile, connections, credits, billing, team, and more.

## The dashboard home

The home page gives you an at-a-glance view:

- **KPI cards** with your key marketing metrics and their trend.
- **Action cards** that jump straight into the Generator, Copier, or Instagram content.
- **A brand setup checklist** that shows how complete each brand is.
- **A tools rail** for one-click access to every tool.
- **Recent runs** so you can pick up where you left off.

## The brand switcher

Your active brand is shown in the header. Click it to switch between brands. The Generator, Copier, and other tools use the active brand's name, industry, logo, and colors automatically.

## The command palette

Press **Ctrl + K** (Windows and Linux) or **⌘ + K** (Mac) from any page to open the command palette. You can also click the **Quick actions** search bar in the header.

The palette lets you:

- Jump to any page without using the sidebar.
- Open or create a brand.
- Launch a tool, schedule a post, or create a folder.
- Open any settings section in one keystroke.

Results filter as you type. Use the arrow keys to move, **Enter** to select, and **Escape** to close. It is available on all plans at no credit cost.

## Notifications

The **bell icon** in the top-right header lights up with a red dot when something you started is ready, such as an Instagram visual finishing in the background. It keeps your last 20 events. A toast also appears in the bottom-right of any screen so you never miss a result. See [Notifications](/account/notifications) for details.

## Theme

Use the theme control in **Settings → Appearance** to switch between light, dark, and system, and to pick a color preset. Changes apply instantly.
