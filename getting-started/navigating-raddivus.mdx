You access every Raddivus feature from a single dashboard. Here is how to move around it.

## The sidebar

The left sidebar is your main navigation. You can collapse it on desktop, and on mobile it opens as a drawer.

- **Home** opens your starting page: the chat box, suggestions, and your setup checklist or numbers.
- **Community** opens the shared feed.
- **Tools** groups the creative and analysis tools: Agent, Ad Library, Image (Ad Generator), Copier, Ad Analytics, Video, IG Content, and Saved Ads.
- **Updates** shows shipped, in-progress, and planned features.
- **Settings** opens your profile, connections, credits, billing, team, and more.

## Home

Home is the first page you see. It greets you by name and is built around the brand you are looking at.

### The chat box

Type a question or a request and press Send to hand it to the [AI Agent](/ai-agent/overview) (Professional and VIP). The **+** menu adds files or images, mentions a brand kit document or a saved ads folder, turns on web research, and holds the Meta Ads switch for that conversation. The model chip picks the Agent's model, the same choice as on the Agent page.

### Suggested for you

Up to three suggestions from your brand's weekly Raddivus analysis. **Try this** puts a suggestion into the chat box, ready to send; once you send it, it counts as applied. You can see and dismiss every suggestion in Ad Analytics → Suggestions.

**Refresh**, beside the heading, runs the analysis now instead of waiting for Monday. It is free and works once per brand per day. On a team, one person's refresh uses the day for everyone on that brand. Once it is used, or when there was nothing new to suggest, the button stays greyed out: point at it, or tap it on a phone, to see when the next refresh is. A refresh that fails does not use up the day. If Meta has logged you out, the button says **Reconnect Meta to refresh** and opens Connections.

### Get your first ad live

Until your brand is ready, Home shows a short checklist. Each step is ticked from what you have actually done:

1. **Connect an ad account.**
2. **Add your brand kit**: your logo, brand colors, and a heading and body font.
3. **Describe the business**: your services, plus your voice, tone, or audience.
4. **Generate your first ad** with the Ad Generator.

One step is open at a time, with the button that takes you there. **Skip for now** moves to the next step; it is shared with your team for that brand, and a skipped step still counts as not done.

While the checklist is unfinished, a **Setup Guide** line in the sidebar shows the same count on every page. Click it to go back to Home.

### Your numbers

When all four steps are done, the checklist and the Setup Guide go away and Home shows three numbers from your connected Meta ad account for the brand: spend, total results, and a third you choose (cost per result, cost per click, or click rate). Each shows its change against the previous period of the same length. Pick the period above the numbers: Today, Past 7 days, Past 30 days, Past 90 days, or This year.

Home costs no credits on any plan. Sending a message from the chat box costs what an Agent message costs.

## The brand switcher

Your active brand is shown in the header. Click it to switch between brands. The Generator, Copier, and other tools use the active brand's name, industry, logo, and colors automatically.

## The command palette

Press **Ctrl + K** (Windows and Linux) or **⌘ + K** (Mac) from any page to open the command palette. You can also click the **Quick actions** search bar in the header.

The palette lets you:

- Jump to any page without using the sidebar.
- Open or create a brand.
- Launch a tool, schedule a post, or create a folder.
- Open any settings section in one keystroke.

Results filter as you type. Use the arrow keys to move, **Enter** to select, and **Escape** to close. It is available on all plans at no credit cost.

## Notifications

The **bell icon** in the top-right header shows a status indicator (a red dot) when something you started is ready, such as an Instagram visual finishing in the background. It keeps your last 20 events. A toast also appears in the bottom-right of any screen so you never miss a result. See [Notifications](/account/notifications) for details.

## Theme

Use the theme control in **Settings → Appearance** to switch between light, dark, and system, and to pick a color preset. Changes apply instantly.
