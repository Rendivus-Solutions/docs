Raddivus runs on a simple model: a monthly subscription gives you a credit allowance, and AI generations spend credits. This page is the quick overview. For the full breakdown, see [Credits & billing](/account/credits-and-billing).

## The free trial

Every new account starts with a **7-day free trial**:

- **10 trial credits**, granted once (not renewable).
- Access to **Professional-plan features**.
- Ends after 7 days or once you use the 10 trial credits, whichever comes first.
- No credit card required to start.

After the trial you need a paid plan to keep generating. You can still browse the app, view saved ads, and manage brands.

Yearly plans are billed once for the year and give you **2 months free** compared with paying monthly.

<Note>
  Team collaboration is VIP-only. VIP is limited to 30 members and adds 1-on-1 ad reviews, strategy calls, private Slack, and early feature access.
</Note>

## How credits work

- You receive your plan's credits each billing cycle.
- **Unused monthly credits roll over** with no cap, so your balance keeps growing.
- You can buy **top-up packs** anytime, and **purchased credits never expire**.
- When you run out, generation is blocked until you top up or your next cycle starts.
- If a generation fails, the credits are **refunded automatically**.
