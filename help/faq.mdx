---

<Info>
  These are the most common questions. For specific errors, see [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
</Info>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can the agent accidentally break my campaigns?">
    No. Every modification (pausing, resuming, changing budgets, uploading ads)
    requires you to click Approve in the chat before anything changes. The agent
    proposes, and you decide. If you click Reject, nothing happens.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work with Google Ads?">
    Not currently. Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) are supported; Google Ads
    is on the roadmap.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I run out of credits?">
    Generation is blocked until you buy more credits or your next billing cycle
    adds new ones. In the agent, it notifies you and the conversation pauses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do unused credits expire?">
    Monthly credits roll over indefinitely with no cap on your balance.
    Purchased credits from top-up packs never expire.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does 1 credit per variation mean?">
    Each unique ad image that gets generated costs 1 credit. Four variations in
    the Ad Copier costs 4 credits, regardless of how many ad types or psychology
    angles you select.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between the Ad Copier and the Ad Generator?">
    The Copier takes an existing ad image (yours or a competitor's) and makes
    branded variations of it, keeping the style. The Generator creates ads from
    scratch based on your offer, brand, and creative choices, with no source
    image.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Raddivus and Raddivus Pro?">
    Same intelligence and tools. Pro can think through problems visibly, create
    step-by-step plans with live status, and delegate research to a faster
    parallel assistant. The Raddivus tier gives the same quality faster and at a
    lower cost per message, without the planning and thinking features.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch model tiers mid-conversation?">
    Yes. The tier selector is in the chat header and can be changed at any time.
    Start with Lite for a quick lookup, then switch to Pro for a deeper analysis
    in the same session.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I upload generated ads directly to Meta?">
    Yes. From the agent chat, ask it to upload a generated ad to a specific ad
    set. You approve an approval card, and the ad is created in your Meta
    account in paused status. You activate it from Meta Ads Manager when you are
    ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if an AI generation fails?">
    Credits are refunded automatically. You can try again without losing
    credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the agent remember me between conversations?">
    Yes. It keeps a short set of notes about you and about each brand and
    carries them into every new chat, so you do not repeat yourself. The
    transcripts of older chats are not carried over, only the notes. See
    [What the agent remembers](/ai-agent/memory).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I see what it remembers, and can I change it?">
    Settings, Memory. Both sets of notes are shown as plain text and you can
    edit them directly. It is a text box, not a locked list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if it writes down something wrong?">
    Edit the text, or clear that set of notes. Both take effect immediately and
    the agent uses the corrected version from the next message on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if it drops something I wanted it to keep?">
    Every rewrite is saved in full. Open "See what changed" in Settings, Memory
    and restore the earlier version in one click. We keep the last 20 versions
    of each set of notes and 90 days of history.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I turn memory off? Does that delete what is already saved?">
    You can turn it off for everything, for "About you", or for one brand at a
    time. Turning it off deletes nothing. The notes stay readable and the agent
    stops adding to them and stops using them. Deleting is a separate action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does memory cost credits?">
    No. Writing, reading, editing, restoring and deleting are all 0 credits. It
    is part of the AI Agent, so the Professional or VIP plan is needed for the
    agent to keep writing new notes. On Starter your existing notes stay
    readable and you can still edit, restore and delete them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can anyone else see what the agent remembers about me?">
    No. Notes are tied to your account. Team members never see each other's
    notes, and nothing learned about one user is used for another.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can team members see each other's chats?">
    No. Agent conversations are private to the user who created them. Teams
    share brands, saved ads, and folders, but not conversation history.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I try Raddivus before subscribing?">
    Yes. New accounts start with a 7-day free trial that includes 10 trial
    credits and Professional-plan access. No credit card is required to start.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What ad output formats are supported?">
    Five ratios: 1:1 (Feed), 4:5 (Portrait Feed), 9:16 (Story/Reel), 16:9
    (Landscape), and 1.91:1 (Link Ad). Three resolutions: 1K, 2K, and 4K.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
