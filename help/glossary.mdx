Quick definitions for the terms you'll see across Raddivus, with links to the pages that cover each one in depth.

## Plans and credits

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Credit** | The currency for AI generation. Each plan includes a monthly allowance, and generations spend credits. See [Credits & billing](/account/credits-and-billing). |
| **Monthly credits** | The credit allowance included with your plan each billing cycle. Unused monthly credits **roll over** with no cap. |
| **Top-up pack** | A one-time credit purchase. Purchased credits never expire. See [Credits & billing](/account/credits-and-billing). |
| **Rollover** | Unused monthly credits carrying into the next cycle indefinitely. |
| **Free trial** | A 7-day trial with 10 one-time credits and Growth-level features, no credit card required. See [Free trial](/getting-started/free-trial). |

## Brands

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand profile** | Your stored brand identity (name, logo, colors, links) that every tool applies automatically. See [Brand profiles](/brands/brand-profiles). |
| **Knowledge base** | Source material attached to a brand that shapes the AI's writing voice and content. See [Knowledge base](/brands/knowledge-base). |
| **Active brand** | The brand currently selected in the header brand switcher. Tools use the active brand's details. |

## Creating ads

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ad Generator** | The tool (sidebar label **Image**) that creates original ad images from your offer and brand. See [Ad Generator](/create-ads/ad-generator). |
| **Ad Copier** | The tool that makes branded variations of an existing ad image you upload. See [Ad Copier](/create-ads/ad-copier). |
| **Variation** | One generated image. The Generator and Copier cost 1 credit per variation. |
| **Ad Library** | A browsable collection of ads for inspiration that can feed the Copier. See [Ad Library](/analytics/ad-library). |

## AI Agent

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Agent** | A conversational assistant connected to your Meta Ads account that answers questions and proposes changes. See [AI Agent overview](/ai-agent/overview). |
| **Model tiers** | The available agent models: **Raddivus Lite**, **Raddivus**, and **Raddivus Pro**, varying by plan. See [Model tiers](/ai-agent/model-tiers). |
| **Permissions** | Account-level toggles that control what the AI Agent can access and change. See [Permissions](/ai-agent/permissions). |
| **Approval** | A change the AI Agent proposes that takes effect only after you confirm it. See [Approvals](/ai-agent/approvals). |

## Meta and publishing

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Meta Ads account** | The Facebook and Instagram ads account you connect to power analytics and the AI Agent. See [Connect Meta Ads](/connections/connect-meta). |
| **Paused status** | Ads created in your Meta account through Raddivus are uploaded paused, so nothing goes live without your action. |
| **Ad Analytics** | A live gallery of every ad in your connected accounts, with metrics and filters. See [Ad Analytics](/analytics/ad-analytics). |
| **IG Content** | The Instagram workspace and social planner for drafting, scheduling, and publishing posts. See [Instagram content overview](/instagram/overview). |
