title: "Import existing content"
description: "Bring an existing content plan into Raddivus from ClickUp or a spreadsheet. Free on all plans, with a 5-step wizard and up to 500 posts at once."
---

Already have a content plan in ClickUp or a spreadsheet? The **Import** tab brings it into Raddivus in minutes. It is **free** on all plans, with no credits charged.

## Import a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the source">
    Choose ClickUp or a spreadsheet (CSV, XLSX, or XLS).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect or upload">
    ClickUp uses a personal token that is never stored. Spreadsheets are parsed
    in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set defaults">
    Choose the brand and the per-post defaults (post type, category, platform).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your columns">
    Match each source column to a Raddivus field: caption, hashtags, scheduled
    date, slide 1 to N, and more. Common column names are auto-detected, so you
    usually only fix mismatches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and import">
    Check the preview and import up to 500 posts at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens to imported posts

Imported posts land in your calendar as **completed** posts, ready to edit, schedule, or publish exactly like AI-generated ones.

<Tip>
  Importing is a fast way to migrate an existing calendar without re-typing it,
  and it costs nothing.
</Tip>
