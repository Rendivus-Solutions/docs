Instagram content turns your brand knowledge base into finished Instagram posts: carousels, single images, stories, and reel scripts. It includes a full social planner calendar, editing, scheduling, publishing, importing, and a ClickUp export.

**Where:** sidebar → **IG Content**. **Cost:** generation is credit-based (editing, scheduling, publishing, importing, and ClickUp export are free). **Plans:** Single post on Professional and VIP, Plan a month on VIP.

## The social planner calendar

Above the form is a full social-media planner. Switch between **Day**, **Week**, and **Month** views, click any day to draft a post pre-scheduled for that date, and filter by platform. Instagram is fully supported. Facebook and Google My Business appear as filters, with their generators planned.

## Plans and credits

- **Single post:** Professional and VIP.
- **Plan a month:** VIP only.
- **Editing, scheduling, publishing, importing, and ClickUp export:** free, no credits.
- Generating a post spends credits based on the work involved. See [Credits & billing](/account/credits-and-billing).

<Tip>
  Posts are grounded in the brand's [knowledge base](/brands/knowledge-base).
  The more source material a brand has, the more on-voice the output.
</Tip>
