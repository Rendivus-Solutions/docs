Plan a month lets you generate a whole editorial calendar at once. You pick the posting days and the content mix, and the AI plans and writes everything.

**Where:** sidebar → **IG Content** (planner). **Cost:** based on each post. **Plans:** VIP only.

Up to a 90-day range and a maximum of 60 posts per plan.

## Plan a calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Plan tab">
    The Plan tab appears on the IG Content page for VIP accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date range">
    Pick a start and end date (up to 90 days out). Quick patterns like "every
    weekday" or "Mon / Wed / Fri" speed this up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the mix">
    Choose the share of carousels, single images, stories, and videos, plus the
    category mix. A live estimate shows the credit cost as you adjust.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the preview">
    The AI returns an editorial summary and a per-post breakdown (topic, pillar,
    style, and angle). Review it before committing.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and generate">
    Approve the plan and every post generates in the background. The calendar
    shows each post's status as it progresses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## While it runs

You can leave the page. The calendar fills in with status badges (color-coded and text-labeled, such as Drafting and Ready) as posts move from drafting to ready. When everything finishes, the plan is marked complete.

<Note>
  You can have one active plan per brand at a time. If a plan stalls, it is
  automatically marked failed after a short window so you can retry.
</Note>
