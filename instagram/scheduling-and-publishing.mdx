---
title: "Schedule & publish"
description: "Publish an Instagram post now with one click, or schedule it for later on the calendar. Both are free and work even before Instagram is connected."
---

Every generated post has two actions next to **Edit**: **Post now** and **Schedule**. Both are **free** and available on Growth and VIP.

## Post now

Click **Post now** to publish straight to your connected Instagram account in one click. This requires a [connected Instagram account](/connections/connect-instagram).

## Schedule for later

Click **Schedule**, pick a date and time, and the post is queued. It then appears on the social planner calendar with a "Scheduled" badge.

<Note>
  If Instagram is not connected yet, scheduling still works. Your post is saved
  as a draft with a date attached, and a banner links you to the Connections
  tab. Once you connect, drafts can be promoted to live scheduled posts.
</Note>

## The social planner calendar

The calendar above the form shows your scheduled content:

- Switch between **Day**, **Week**, and **Month** views.
- Click any day to draft a new post pre-scheduled for that date.
- Filter by platform. Instagram is live today, with Facebook and Google My Business coming next.

## Troubleshooting

  <Accordion title="Post now is disabled">

    Connections](/connections/connect-instagram). Publishing also requires the
    Growth or VIP plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
