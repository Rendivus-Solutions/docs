Every generated post has two actions next to **Edit**: **Post now** and **Schedule**. Both are available on Professional and VIP.

<Note>
  Publishing to Instagram is rolling out account by account while our Meta app approval completes. If **Post now** or **Schedule** is not yet enabled on your account, contact support and we will switch it on for you.
</Note>

<Info>
  **Post now** and **Schedule** are free actions and never use extra credits.
  Generating the post content is what costs credits. See [Credits and
  billing](/account/credits-and-billing) for the full breakdown.
</Info>

## Post now

Click **Post now** to publish straight to your connected Instagram account in one click. This requires a [connected Instagram account](/connections/connect-instagram).

## Schedule for later

Click **Schedule**, pick a date and time, and the post is queued. It then appears on the social planner calendar with a "Scheduled" badge.

Five minutes before a scheduled post goes out, the person who scheduled it gets a preview email with a **Cancel** link (the account owner gets it if that person has left the team). The post publishes on time unless someone cancels it.

<Note>
  Scheduling needs a connected Instagram account. Without one you can still
  save the post as a draft, and schedule it once the account is connected.
</Note>

## Which Instagram account publishes

A post publishes through the Instagram account connected to its brand by anyone on your team, so it still goes out when the person who wrote it is away. A post set to a specific account only publishes there. If you disconnect the account a brand publishes through and nobody else on the team has that brand connected, its scheduled posts are marked as needing a reconnect. The posts are kept.

## Post an image you already have

To schedule or publish a saved ad, a Generator or Copier image, or an upload, see [Post your own images](/instagram/post-your-own-images).

## The social planner calendar

The calendar above the form shows your scheduled content:

- Switch between **Day**, **Week**, and **Month** views.
- Click any day to draft a new post pre-scheduled for that date.
- Filter by platform. Instagram is live today, with Facebook and Google My Business coming next.
