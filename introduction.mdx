---
title: "What is Raddivus"
description: "Raddivus is an AI marketing copilot that generates on-brand ad creative and Instagram content and analyzes your Meta Ads, no agency required."
---

Raddivus is a marketing platform for small businesses and solopreneurs. It connects to your Meta Ads account and gives you an AI copilot that can analyze your campaigns, generate ad images and videos, draft Instagram content, and make account changes, all from one place. It also includes standalone tools for creating ads from scratch or from a competitor's ad, with your brand applied automatically.

The platform handles the creative and analytical work that most small businesses either skip or outsource. You get AI-generated creative, campaign performance insights, and the ability to act on those insights without a media buyer or a designer.
