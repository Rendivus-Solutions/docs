This guide takes you from a brand-new account to your first ads. It takes about five minutes, and your first three ads are free.

Every new account starts with a **7-day free trial** with **10 trial credits** and full feature access. No credit card is required. The trial ends after 7 days or once you use all 10 trial credits, whichever comes first.

## Set up your account

Setup is seven short screens. You can close the tab at any point and come back where you left off.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Choose **Continue with Google**, or type your full name and email. Tick the box agreeing to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then press **Continue**. With email, we send you a 6-digit code: type it on the "Check your email" screen (or tap the button in the email). No password is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your profile">
    Add a photo, your display name and a username. The username is required: it is how teammates find you, and we suggest a free one made from your name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your brand">
    Add your brand's name and logo, and its website if it has one. Once the name is in, set your colours, fonts, services and location in the brand kit. With a website, we read it and fill these in for you; change anything that is off. You can skip this step and add a brand later from **Brands**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace">
    The workspace is the account your team shares. Name it, say how you will use Raddivus, and invite teammates by email if you like (see [Team workspace](/collaborate/team)). Optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us where you found us">
    One tap, or skip it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See your first three ads">
    As soon as you save your brand, we start making three free ads for it, in your colours, fonts and logo. They are usually ready by the time you get here. These cost no credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your plan">
    Choose the plan your trial runs on: Starter, Professional or VIP. Nothing is charged. Picking **Professional** claims **50% off your first month**, or **20% off your first year** if you pay annually (see [Free trial](/getting-started/free-trial)). **Skip for now** finishes without choosing. You land on your brands.
  </Step>
</Steps>

New accounts open in light mode. Switch any time from your profile menu or **Settings**.

## Generate your next ad

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Ad Generator">
    Open **Image (Ad Generator)** from the sidebar. Pick your brand, choose an offer (for example "Free estimate" or a custom one), pick a format and number of variations, then click **Generate ads**.

    Your ads appear as they finish, usually within 30 to 120 seconds. Each variation costs 1 credit.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or download the result">
    Hover an ad and tick its checkbox to select it. A selection bar lets you **save** it to a folder, **download** it (single image or a ZIP for several), **share** it to the Ad Library, or **edit** it with a follow-up prompt.
  </Step>
</Steps>
